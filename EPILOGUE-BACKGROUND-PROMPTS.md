**How to use these**
- Every prompt is the "describe it" kind (Option 2). Attach **only** the world's two style pictures, then paste the prompt. They're all in `Place Prompt Attachments`:
  - HOAX: `BG - style 1 (HOAX).png` and `BG - style 2 (HOAX).png`
  - strangers: `BG - style 1 (Strangers).png` and `BG - style 2 (Strangers).png`
  - Stay: each item lists its own attachments.
- **Night versions:** make the day one first. Then attach **your new day picture first** and the two style pictures after it, and paste the night prompt. It keeps the room the same and changes only the light.
- **Save as** the name given (PNG is fine) and drop it in New Adds. Claude swaps it in. Names that already exist in the game replace the old picture; a "(new)" name gets wired in as an extra.
- **If a picture comes out too evenly lit or beige**, add this to the end of the prompt and run it again:
  `More contrast: strong directional light from the real light sources, glowing highlights, soft deep shadows in the corners, warm golden tone with cool blue at the windows, like the attached images.`
- **HOAX** runs from just before Christmas 2023 to mid-January 2024 in Hanlim, an ordinary Korean town (the book never names it): snow on the ground, grey-white winter light by day, deep-blue snowy nights, Christmas lights still up.
- **strangers** runs from mid-February to mid-March 2013 in Incheon: the end of a hard winter. There's old snow in the corners, frozen ground, grey sea light, wind off the Yellow Sea, and by March, yellow dust. Everything should look like 2013 (no newer phones, cars or signs).
- Nothing should have readable writing.
