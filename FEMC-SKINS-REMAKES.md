Everything here is **already in the game** and works as it is. These are the same prompts that made those pieces, rewritten with each look's new **look bible** (its own palette, motifs and light) instead of the old shared pink-and-black. Make only the ones you want redone; skip the rest.

How: same as the design sheet. New ChatGPT message, attach the files on the **Attach** line in that order (all inside `Femc Mode Images/`), paste the prompt, save the picture into **New Adds** under its **Save as** name (the same name as before), and tell Claude: the new one replaces the old one.

Covered: Solar's 32 looks (Theurgy cut-in, finish card, entrance card and both "1 MORE!" pictures: v1 the sideways glance, v2 looking front) and Haechan's five made looks (Sweater Date, Headphones On, Arrivals Gate, Window Seat, Bad Influence: Theurgy cut-in, finish card, entrance card, "1 MORE!" strip). **180 prompts.** Their second pose sheets have no colours or effects in them, so they don't need remaking.
