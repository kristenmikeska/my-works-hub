# Runners: Haechan's and Mark's families (lore reference)

*Gathered Nov 5 2026 from Kat's what-if **Better Photography** (`~/Claude/Projects/Runners Writing Archive/Long Form Series/Better Photography Series/`). Kat said on Nov 5 2026 that the game may use it ("Yes, use it"). Her own rulings below come first, the book's scholarship story wins over Better Photography's, and her name stays **Hyesook**, not Miyoung.*

## Kat's rulings (canon for the game)
- **Haechan's mother:**
  - She's **Hyesook** in the game. Kat's name for her is Miyeon, and Better Photography uses Miyoung Park, but strangers already has a Miyeon.
  - She is **not ill**. She is very emotionally abusive and has an alcoholism problem.
  - Haechan is somewhat estranged from her.
- **Hyein's surname is Lee.**

## From Better Photography (approved for the game)
**Where it is:**
- **pt25 `theonlyroomsheliked`:** Haechan tells Solar about his childhood. This is the best single source.
- **pt24 `fourminutes`:** his mother's apartment in December, and the Lees' house afterwards.
- **pt33–34:** his mother at the Lees' house, and him moving his things out.
- **pt38 `twoninetyone`:** a letter in his mother's voice.
- **pt9–11:** the scholarship incident (the AU version).

**Haechan's mother:**
- **Surname:** Park, not Lee. In pt33 Solar finds this out from "Mrs. Park".
- **Work and family:** she has worked the same four jobs in rotation since she was 19. She has a sister in Palisades Park.
- **The pattern:**
  - The first ten minutes are warm and funny, then it turns.
  - Money comes first: the rent, what he cost, "your free ride".
  - Then she counts his missed birthdays and Christmases. Sometimes it goes past talking.
- **Drinking:** in his words, "drinks sometimes and sometimes she doesn't". Her letter says six years, mostly stopped.
- **Singing:** at 8 he sang to her in the kitchen. She told him to stop because it was his father's face doing it, and he never sang again. That's why there's no voice on his tracks.
- **What she says:** "She'll find out what you are eventually."

**Haechan's home:**
- **His father:** left when he was 8 (the letter says 2). He's alive, and Haechan has never looked for him.
- **The apartment:** a 4th-floor walk-up in Fort Lee, NJ. The front door is propped with a brick. Mrs. Yoon lives on the second-floor landing.
- **Money:** "No money. Not tight, no money." He worked at a pizza place on Main Street at 16. He's at Syracuse on a scholarship.

**The Lees (Mark's family), four streets up the hill:**
- **Who they are:**
  - **Dongwook:** affection is a hand on the back of the neck. He asks for help by stating a fact: "There's a light out in the garage."
  - **Jiyoung:** "Have you eaten?" She leaves galbijjim in the oven on low and labels food containers with masking tape.
  - **Their home:** comfortable, not rich. A house on a hill with a hoop over the garage. Mark seems to be an only child.
- **Taking Haechan in:**
  - From 12 or 13 he went to Mark's whenever it got bad. Jiyoung "just started buying more food".
  - He has a kept room with a sloped ceiling: the bed made, a charger plugged in since 2019.
  - Mark has never once gone up to the apartment: "he has to watch me look at it."

**Mark and Haechan:**
- They met at 12 or 13 over a song written on a worksheet.
- After that it was six years in Mark's room with a laptop and a $40 audio interface.

**How it differs from the book:**
- **The scholarship:** Better Photography's version (Mark used Haechan's material against Danielle) isn't the book's.
- **Solar's family:** in Exhibition, Visiting Hours and Home Turf, "Mr./Mrs. Lee" means Solar's parents.
- **Ages:** Better Photography itself disagrees on his mother's age (51 or 49), and on when they met (12 or 13).
