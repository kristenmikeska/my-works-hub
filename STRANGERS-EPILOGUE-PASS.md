# strangers epilogue: the expansion pass (Nov 5-6 2026)

You asked for the same improvements the Runners epilogue got, for the other epilogues. This page covers strangers. I've kept out what happens in the new scenes so they stay a surprise. Nothing is committed or pushed.

## The short version

- **More to play:** about **33,700 more words**. That counts every narration, line, text and choice on every branch, and takes the act from about 108,400 words to about 142,100.
- **New scenes:** **3 new story scenes** and **17 new hangouts**, plus 8 new texts.
- **More free time:**
  - **Before:** 38 free Daytime / Evening times and no Late Nights. Two of those times had nobody to see.
  - **Now:** **49 free times**, every one with someone to see.
  - **Per run:** about **42 hangouts** on offer, up from about 28.
- strangers had the opposite problem to Runners. It had plenty of time but not enough people to spend it with, so most of this pass is new scenes rather than new time.

## What changed

| | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Late Night** | On 13 quiet nights, Late Night now lists the people still up above Sleep. A late hangout uses the night, then she goes to bed. Nights with their own story scene stay as they were. |
| **The empty evenings** | The two evenings that had nobody to see now have someone. |
| **A party** | One new story scene is a long party with the whole house in it. |
| **More time with the house** | More with the kitchen, Yunjin, both parents, Riki and the girls. |
| **More of both boys on every route** | Each boy has more scenes that play whichever route you're on, not only his own. |
| **Each route** | Each route gets one new late-night scene of its own. |
| **The weather now matches the story** | Every day of the act has fixed weather that fits what the scenes describe. Before, only eight days were fixed and the rest was random. |
| **The phone** | Eight new texts that fit their week. The quietest days now have something. |
| **Small recurring touches** | The fish, the thermos, the calendar on the kitchen wall, the radio, Mr. Oh's driving and Auntie Song's Mandarin all carry through the new scenes. |
