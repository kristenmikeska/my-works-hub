# strangers epilogue: the expansion passes (Nov 5-6 2026)

You asked for the same improvements the Runners epilogue got, and then on Nov 6 you answered the questions and said yes to five ideas and to more heat. This page covers both passes. I've kept out what happens in the new scenes so they stay a surprise. Nothing is committed or pushed.

## The short version

| | Before the first pass | After the first pass (Nov 5-6) | After the second pass (Nov 6) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Words (every narration, line, text and choice on every branch) | about 108,400 | about 142,100 | about **175,800** |
| Story scenes | 38 | 41 | **43** |
| Hangouts written | 33 | 50 | **61** |
| Hangouts on offer per run | about 28 | about 42 | about **50-52** |
| Free Daytime / Evening times | 38 | 36 | **34** (two afternoons went to the new story scenes) |
| Late Nights with people up | 0 | 13 | **13** (as you asked) |
| Texts | 52 | 60 | **70**, plus two that arrive the morning after a scene |

So strangers now has more to do than there is time to do it in, the way Runners does. You won't see everything in one run.

## What the second pass added

| | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Both boys in one car** | Two new story scenes that put Haechan and Jungwon in the same car with Solar, one before the route locks and one after. The second is written three times, once per route, so it plays differently depending on whose story you're on. This is the biggest thing in the pass. |
| **The girls, everywhere** | Chaewon, Sakura, Kazuha and Eunchae each have a hangout of their own now, and the committee has two more nights out together, one of them in Daeboreum week. They also turn up in the group chat more, at the party (from a distance), and by the gate once. |
| **More heat** | Three new route scenes (two on Haechan's, one on Jungwon's), heat in the car scenes on both routes, a little more in one older late night, and a morning after. Everything still cuts away at the door, the way the book's pages do in the game. |
| **Two more late nights** | One in a car at the gate, and one in a kitchen across the lane. |
| **Your fixes** | Auntie Song goes home every night now. Wherever she used to sleep in her armchair, she catches the last bus or gets a lift home instead, and the people who live in the house have the kitchen at night. |
| **The phone** | Ten more texts, mostly the committee's chat, and new lines on several contact cards that unlock from the new scenes. |
