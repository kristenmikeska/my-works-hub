# Persona 3: Equinox · UI directions (mockup)

Four candidate looks for the sequel, built as static demos (Nov 15 2026). Open `index.html` to compare them; each direction page shows the same five screens (A title, B field menu, C dialogue with two choices, D battle commands with the party strip, E one Equinox-only widget) at 16:9 and at phone width. Shared pieces: `equinox.css` (page shell, 16:9 frames sized in `cqw`, phone column fallback under 720px, reduced motion) and `equinox.js` (fills the lists and party strip, ↑↓ / Home / End / Enter / hover / click navigation on every `[data-nav]` list, dialogue typing; events `eq:select` and `eq:pick`). All four keep Solstice's Reload shapes (bold italic slanted lists, a selection shape behind the chosen row, paper tags, angular plates, ghost words) and stay inside Kat's palette rule: no blue, navy, indigo, purple, lilac or green UI, gold only as a speck. Fonts come from Google Fonts with system fallbacks; no external scripts. Not wired to the game.

**1 · Halfway (day = night).** Palette: bone `#f4ede8` (bone-2 `#e6dcd6`), ink `#0e0709` (ink-2 `#1c1014`), seam pink `#ff4f9e` / `#ff8cc0` (Solstice's own pink, the family link), ash `#8c7f86`. Type: Barlow Condensed italic 800-900 for titles and lists, Barlow Semi Condensed for body, Cormorant Garamond italic for small lines. Shapes: every screen split down the middle (bone left, black right) with a 3px hot-pink seam that glows; two-tone words (a 90° gradient clipped to the letters: ink on the bone half, bone on the black half) for the logo, date and title menu; menus as two mirrored columns that lean toward the seam (left column right-aligned, right column left-aligned, mirrored clip shapes); a diamond knot on the seam follows the chosen row; the sun-and-moon half-disc mark (sun with rays left, crescent right, pink seam through it); choices mirrored either side of the seam; diamond-cropped party faces. Motion: the logo's letters close in from wide spacing, pink bars grow from the seam side, rays turn slowly, the dialogue's night twin slides in from the right. Phones: the seam turns sideways (bone top, black bottom) and lists stack in one column. Widget: a 24-hour day / night dial (18:00 at the top, the day half on the left, a pink hand), a slider and Play / Pause.

**2 · The Well (descent).** Palette: abyss `#070204`, blood `#2a0509`, crimson `#c8102e`, blood-rose `#ff3b6b`, foam `#ffd9e0`, silt `#8a5a64`. Type: Big Shoulders Display 700-900 (tall, narrow, all caps, tabular depth numbers), Barlow Semi Condensed body, Spectral italic for small lines. Shapes: a background that darkens to blood toward the bottom, drifting pink caustics, faint horizontal strata, a depth ruler down the right edge of every screen (the field menu's ruler follows the chosen row), a glowing water line with expanding elliptical ripples, outline ghost words set vertically, lists that step further right as they go down (each row a level deeper, with depth sub-labels), a crimson fill that wipes in behind the chosen row and a ring on the water beside it. The logo's letters step down one by one with a foam-to-crimson fill and a wavering reflection under the water line. The dialogue box has a wavy animated water edge; the name tag hangs into it. Motion: chosen text sinks in (drops from above with a blur and settles), choices drop in, ripples spread, bubbles rise. Widget: the depth gauge (a vertical shaft filling crimson to a foam water line, metres and Block / floor readout, a note per block, a block list and Ascend / Descend).

**3 · Red Thread (the red string of fate).** Palette: paper `#f5ece4`, paper-2 `#ebdfd4`, ink `#17100f`, thread `#e8336d` (light `#ff7aa2`), pencil `#8a7d78`, tape `rgba(243, 211, 220, .78)`. Type: Barlow Condensed italic 900 in black ink for titles and lists, Barlow Semi Condensed body, Caveat for handwritten notes in thread red or pencil. Shapes: ruled paper with a dashed pink stitch inset around every screen, tape strips, polaroid portraits, paper-slip choices, the chosen row turning into a slanted ink tag. The thread is one SVG path per screen, redrawn on every selection through each `[data-knot]` in order plus the chosen row of the list marked `data-knot-at` (logo → spool → row; photo → row → meter; photo → choice → name tag; leader → command → Shadow), hanging with a little sag, with a knot dot on the chosen row. HP / SP bars are thread over a dashed pencil line. Motion: the thread redraws itself (stroke dash) each time you move, the spool turns, the chosen knot pulses on the meter. Widget: the Thread meter (one string across the screen, a knotted segment per group sized by its share, the chosen group's segment sags and pulses, the loose end dashed "still loose", total out of 400 and this month's gain).

**4 · Mirror (reflections).** Palette: void `#0b0809`, chrome `linear-gradient(#fff, #f6e8ee 38%, #8d7a82 50%, #f1dce4 62%, #fff)`, rose-silver `#d9b7c2`, silver `#b9a3ac`, smoke `#6e5d64`, sheen pink `#ff9cc4`, glass `rgba(255, 236, 243, .06)` with a `rgba(255, 214, 228, .28)` edge. Type: Syncopate 700 (wide caps) for the logo and headings, Archivo condensed (wdth 75) italic 800-900 for lists, Archivo for body. Shapes: chrome text (gradient clipped to letters); every heading stands on its own reflection (`-webkit-box-reflect`, Chrome / Safari); a thin rose-silver floor line; glass plates with backdrop blur and angular clips; floating glass shards; Solar beside a grey mirrored twin split by a crack line; the chosen row becomes a chrome plate whose crack lines spread out from a point. Motion: a slow pink sheen sweeps across each screen, shards drift, the logo resolves from blur, cracks spread on selection. Widget: the reflection card (Solar above a mirror line, her reflection below; Look / Look closer / Touch the glass shift the reflection out of step, tint it pink and crack the glass), with a chrome info panel.
