STUDIO 4B

The mix

Four faders, your song. Play it back and he'll tell you the one thing that's wrong. Fewer playbacks is better.

STUDIO 4B

The booth

Sing the song for real, rhythm-game style. Arrows rise to the targets in time with the voice: D F J K (← ↓ ↑ →), hold the trails. Esc pauses (speed, downscroll, offset).

Make my own chart: the song plays with your current chart dimmed. Tap D F J K along with her voice (hold for long notes); whatever you play over gets replaced. On duets, press 1 when Solar starts singing, 2 for Haechan, 3 for both. Mess up? Esc → Back 5 sec and play over it again. Esc → Finish & save when you're done, even halfway: the rest stays.
