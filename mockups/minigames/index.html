Epilogue minigames

Prototypes. None of these are in the story yet. Try them, then tell Claude what to change.

REGIONALS · 12/6–7

Support

Keep Syracuse alive for 70 seconds. Tap teammates (1–4) to heal, Space for the Suzu on the callouts, Q for Kitsune Rush.

NEW YEAR'S EVE · 12/31

Dumplings

Roll the wrapper, spoon the filling, pleat it shut, then pan-fry them golden. Four dumplings. Haechan is grading.

STUDIO 4B

The mix

Four faders, your song. Play it back and he'll tell you the one thing that's wrong. Fewer playbacks is better.

STUDIO 4B

The booth

Sing the song for real, rhythm-game style. Arrows rise to the targets in time with the voice: D F J K (← ↓ ↑ →), hold the trails. Esc pauses (speed, downscroll, offset).

Make my own chart: the song plays with your current chart dimmed. Tap D F J K along with her voice (hold for long notes); whatever you play over gets replaced. On duets, press 1 when Solar starts singing, 2 for Haechan, 3 for both. Mess up? Esc → Back 5 sec and play over it again. Esc → Finish & save when you're done, even halfway: the rest stays.

FIFTH AVENUE · 12/29, 2 A.M.

Listen

He names a sound of the city. Tap the lights on the street to hear them, then pick the one he means.

GLITCH NIGHTS · DEMO · HORROR

Hold on

2 a.m., and the room is losing track of her. Hold (mouse, finger or Space) when it pulls, let go in the quiet to breathe, and while holding rest on the glowing things to ground her. Not all of them are real: watch for the wrong colour, a pulse out of step, a word misspelled, a sound a beat late. Esc pauses.

Psychological horror. Flashing lights (tick Reduce flashes to soften them).

STRANGERS · INCHEON · DEMO

Hwatu

Go-Stop at the family table against her mother (or add an auntie). Match a card to one of the same month, flip from the pile, build sets, then decide: Go or Stop. Rules on R, Esc pauses.

STRANGERS · THE FISH MARKET · DEMO

The fish market

Read the tide chart and be on the quay when the boat lands, then haggle down Mrs. Bang's list: judge each fish (eyes, gills, flesh), push the price without losing the seller. Esc pauses.

STRANGERS · STRANGE NIGHTS · DEMO · HORROR

The seventh stair

Down the dark stairs to the kitchen and back to bed. Move only while the house is loud enough to cover you; freeze when it goes quiet. Some of the sounds aren't real. Count the stairs. Esc pauses.

Psychological horror. Flashing lights (tick Reduce flashes to soften them). Every sound has a caption.

HOAX · YEOBO'S · DEMO

The café

A shift at Sunoo's grandmother's café. Take the order (a ticket prints onto the rail), then switch stations with the tabs: pour drinks to the line, flip hotteok and toast at the right doneness, roll and slice kimbap and add toppings, then hand over the tray. Every step is scored; tips buy upgrades between rushes. Sunoo can work a station for you. Esc pauses.

STRANGERS · THE BADUK BOARD · DEMO

Baduk puzzles

Capture puzzles on a small board, Black to play: atari, ladders, nets, snapbacks, making eyes and killing them. A wrong move gets answered; try again. Pass skips one. Esc pauses.

HOAX · DEMO · HORROR

The planchette

A spirit board by candlelight. The group asks questions and agrees on an answer; drag the planchette (or arrows, Space to hold steady) and rest on each letter to spell it, then GOODBYE. Something else is pushing. Not every letter is where it says it is. Esc pauses.

Psychological horror. Flashing lights, sudden darkness (tick Reduce flashes to soften them). Every sound has a caption.

HOAX · HANLIM PARK · DEMO

The Trip

Mushrooms in the park at night. Walk (arrows / WASD or hold the mouse), coax each of the crew back from their side quest, and keep the vibe up (the blanket's music, streetlights, water, Nana) or the night curdles. Everyone on the blanket by sunrise. Esc pauses.

HOAX · THE CIRCLE · DEMO

Pass It

Around the rug at Jihoon's. Tap on the beat to pass, hold to take a hit and let go in the pink, and keep up with the interruptions (deep thoughts, who has it, Nana, the chips going the other way). Esc pauses.
