Where you are

Fix a part

  • Drag in the left strip (the waveform) to mark a section. L loops it.
  • Turn on ● Record (R) and press Space. Each pass of the loop wipes that section and records what you tap: D F J K = ← ↓ ↑ →. Hold a key for a long note. Keep looping until a pass feels right, then press Space to stop.
  • Slow it to 0.75× or 0.5× if a part is fast. The pitch stays the same.
  • Listen back with Claps on: every note clicks as it crosses the pink line, so you can hear if it's on her voice.

By hand

  • Click a lane to add a note (it snaps to the grid). Drag down while adding to make it a long note.
  • Click a note to delete it. Delete clears the marked section.
  • The scroll wheel moves through the song while it's paused. [ ] zoom. Home goes to the start.
  • , . nudge the marked section's notes 10 ms earlier / later.

Tap delay

Taps land a little late for most people. If your recorded notes sit after her voice, raise this. ms
