**How to use these**
- Every prompt is the "describe it" kind (Option 2): attach **only** `BG - style 1 (Runners).png` and `BG - style 2 (Runners).png`, then paste the prompt. Both are in `Place Prompt Attachments`.
- **Night versions:** make the day one first, then attach **your new day picture first** and the two style pictures after it, and paste that night prompt. It keeps the room the same and changes only the light.
- **Save as** the name given (PNG is fine) and drop it in New Adds. Claude swaps it in. Names that already exist in the game replace the old picture; a "new" name gets wired in as an extra version.
- **If a picture comes out too evenly lit or beige**, add this to the end of the prompt and run it again:
  `More contrast: strong directional light from the real light sources, glowing highlights, soft deep shadows in the corners, warm golden tone with cool blue at the windows, like the attached images.`
- It's December in Syracuse in every picture: snow on the ground, grey or low winter light by day, and dark blue nights.
- Syracuse's colours are orange and navy blue. The block "S" can appear on banners, but nothing should have readable writing.
