# FEMC Revamp · Link Perks (draft 1, for Kat to review)

Every social link earns named perks as it ranks up, the way Persona 5 Phantom X does it. Bonds pay off in fights, in Tartarus and in Solar's days, and every link ends with something Solar keeps at MAX. Each link gets about four perks, mixing **Battle**, **Tartarus**, **Daily** and a **MAX** skill or passive. Friends who never fight still help from outside, P5-style: they cut in with a portrait pop-up and act without using a turn. Everything here is mechanical. No story, no routes, no scene spoilers. The numbers are starting points for balance testing, and every name and rank can change. Kat's own perks are marked ★.

**How to read the tables**
- **Battle** works when that person is in the active party. **Assist** is a Battle perk from someone outside the party. It works in every fight and shows as a cut-in (following the cut-in setting).
- **Tartarus** covers the node map: floors, chests, clocks, access points, escapes and the Reaper.
- **Daily** covers the life sim: social stats, shops, jobs, condition and free time.
- **MAX** is a skill Solar learns or a passive she keeps. Skills take one of Haenim's 8 slots.
- "Once per battle" resets every fight. "Once per night" resets when the party leaves Tartarus.
- Perks of the same kind stack up to a cap: shop discounts 30%, crit bonuses +30%, and buffs follow the new buff-stacking limit.
- Perks stay earned once unlocked, even when a link can't rank any further. A reversed link pauses its perks until it's repaired.
- Social stats use the new five: **Knowledge** (the old Academics), **Courage**, **Proficiency**, **Charm**, **Kindness**.
- *(needs X)* marks a perk that waits on a system that isn't built yet.
