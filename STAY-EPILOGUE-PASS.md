# Stay epilogue: the days pass (Nov 5 2026)

The same kind of improvements the Runners epilogue got, scaled to Stay (the smallest epilogue: two weeks of spring break in
Iwatodai). Spoiler-free: this page says what kinds of things were added, never what happens in them.

## In numbers

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Story scenes | 16 | 20 |
| Hangouts | 15 | 27 |
| Everyday routines | 5 | 7 |
| Words (scene text) | about 19,200 | about 30,300 |
| Free times with someone to see (Daytime, Evening, Late Night) | 22 Daytime / Evening, no Late Night | 21 Daytime / Evening + 7 Late Nights |
| Hangouts on offer in one run | 14-15 | 24-26 |
| Phone: dated texts / ambient texts | 12 / 5 | 18 / 8 |
| Days with fixed weather | 4 of 14 | 14 of 14 |

Stay had the opposite problem to Runners: plenty of free time, not enough people to spend it with (about a third of a
perfect run's free times only had places on the list). Now nearly every free time has someone in it.

## What kinds of things were added

- **More time with the dorm.** Four new story scenes in the stretches that were quiet, so the days between the big moments
  have the people around you in them.
- **Late nights.** Seven quiet nights now list who's still up, like Runners' late nights. Most of them are with friends,
  and one is the kind of late-night gathering you liked in Runners, a small party that gets bigger.
- **Everyone gets more.** Ken and Aigis had one hangout each; they have three now. Fuuka, Yukari, Junpei and Mitsuru each
  have more, and so do both romance routes (one new late night each). Shinjiro has a new hangout on every route.
- **The last day isn't empty any more.** It used to have nobody to see. Now there are goodbyes to choose between.
- **A small recurring touch.** Koromaru turns up more, across the fortnight, the way Nana does in HOAX.
- **Texts that fit the week.** New texts and phone topics that follow what's going on in the dorm that day.
- **The weather matches the story.** Every day is fixed to what its scenes say (a fair, dry spring; the clouded days are
  the indoor ones).
- **Small fixes.** A few lines that disagreed with each other about days and dates now agree; two hospital places now show
  their own pictures instead of the corridor.
