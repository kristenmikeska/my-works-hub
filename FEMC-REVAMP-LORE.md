## The premise

**Solar is the same girl in three worlds.** She is Solar Lee in *Runners*, the Solar of *strangers*, and Solar Kim in *Hoax*, all at once. She is one person living three lives.

On one night, all three worlds lost her.

She wakes up in **Tartarus** with no memory of who she is or where she came from. She can fight, and she shouldn't be able to. Akihiko finds her. S.E.E.S. takes her in, enrolls her at Gekkoukan, and she becomes one of them.

**Solar is FeMC.** There was no FeMC in this world before her. Every trace of "the protagonist" in P3 starts the night she arrives.

**She wakes up already in a Gekkoukan uniform** (no armband yet). Nothing from any of her three lives is on her, so no single world claims her. **(Kat:)** the black hoodie she sleeps in later (the unknown-text CG) is on purpose: a quiet echo, not a clue that picks a world. The eerie part is that this world made a place for her before she arrived.

## Why she's here: her Shadow

In all three lives, Solar is being pushed toward an ending she didn't choose:
- *Runners*: her father's expectations, and the version of her life that was planned for her.
- *strangers*: a wedding date she didn't pick.
- *Hoax*: the election, and the person everyone needs her to be.

Some part of her wanted out of all three at once. That wish became **her Shadow**: a version of her that tore her out of every life she had and dropped her somewhere nothing of hers has to end. *(Working name: **the Lacuna**, "the gap in a text where something is missing." Solar is the gap in three worlds.)*

The Shadow isn't cruel. It's the part of her that is tired. It keeps offering to let her stay, forget, and never choose. The finale is the Persona moment: *I am thou.* What she does when she faces it decides her ending.

Players shouldn't clock it right away. **Reveal:** in the final phase her cap falls and her hair comes down. She's Solar.

Her charms are broken: a cracked bell, a split jade ring, a bent star pin, headphones with the cord cut.

## The blank page

Elizabeth's compendium has a page for everyone who enters this world. **Solar's page is blank.** Elizabeth has never seen that before, and she is as lost as Solar at first. This is the mystery from day one.

## Her Persona: Haenim (해님, "Lady Sun")

From the Korean tale of the sister who became the sun: she was afraid of being looked at, so she became so bright no one could look at her directly. A girl who hides by shining. *Hae* (sun) is also in Haechan's name.

**Look (art done: `Femc Mode Images/Personas/Haenim.png`):** blush-pink and gold hanbok-inspired gown, sun crown of ribbons and gold pins, a sheer pink veil with a gold sun over her eyes, a white fox mask pushed aside, porcelain doll joints. She carries charms from her three lives: a gold bell (Kiriko and *Runners*), a jade ring on a red cord (*strangers*), a star pin (the *Hoax* campaign), and headphones. She fights with the golden rope from the myth.

**Theurgy:** "Rise and Shine": she lifts her veil and the sun pours out (cut-in done).

**Growth:** she starts veiled and mismatched, and her name glitches when she's summoned. Each route has its own final form:
- *Runners*: **Sunna**, the runner under stage lights (art done: `Personas/Haenim-Sunna.png`).
- *strangers*: **Mazu**, the sea goddess who protects the people waiting on the docks (art done: `Personas/Haenim-Harbor.png`).
- *Hoax*: **Nike**, winged Victory (art done: `Personas/Haenim-Nike.png`).
- **True ending:** Haenim with the veil off, the sun you *can* look at (art done: `Personas/Haenim-Unveiled.png`).

## Who arrives, and why (in this order)

1. **Only Solar sees it at first.** Small impossible things: a song she knows, a smell, a name on the tip of her tongue, things that feel too familiar. Even Elizabeth can't explain them.
2. **Game characters arrive.** Her Shadow's pull caught other people who were running from their own endings (Widowmaker's chair, Judy's Night City, Sebastian's small town...). They are *visitors*. They don't know her.
3. **People from her novels slip in. They are *searchers*.** They know her because she *is* their Solar: Hanni and Hyein, Jaehyun, Minji, Mark, Haechan, Yunjin, Jungwon and his friends, Jihoon and his crew, Cherry. Back home, their Solar is missing, and they followed the hole she left.

**How the searchers get here:**
1. **Her phone number is the first crack.** Solar's old number from each world still connects, faintly. Back home, the people who love her keep texting a phone that should be dead, and sometimes it reaches her. First contact is texts from people she doesn't remember.
2. **Her Persona's charms are the doors.** Each charm on Haenim belongs to one world: the bell to *Runners*, the jade ring to *strangers*, the star pin to *Hoax*. When Solar recovers a memory fragment, that world's charm rings, and people looking hard enough for her hear it and follow. Chasing a world's memories opens the way for its people.
3. **They arrive in the Dark Hour.** A searcher crosses at midnight, awake and missing her. They wake in Iwatodai's green hour and don't become coffins, because they aren't from here. That's how S.E.E.S. finds them.

So the order is: texts, then her memories ring a charm, then someone who misses her steps through at midnight. That's why the searchers come after the game characters: she has to start remembering first.

**The pain at the center:** they know her, and she doesn't know them yet. "It's you. Why don't you know me?"

**Flashbacks:** throughout, Solar gets snippets and recollections from *Runners*, *strangers* and *Hoax*. They're often triggered by something happening in front of her that mirrors her own life.

## Routes and endings

Endings come from choices made across the game: whose fragments she follows, who she's closest to, and what she says at key moments. It is never one choice at the end.

- **Runners**: she goes home to Syracuse.
- **strangers**: she goes home to the Lee house.
- **Hoax**: she goes home to Hanlim.
- **Stay**: she chooses this world, S.E.E.S. and Akihiko. The three worlds go on missing her.
- **True ending (IN; hardest to reach)**: Solar understands her Shadow (Wren) didn't steal her from her lives. Wren is the part of her that couldn't bear choosing. Every other route ends with Solar beating or leaving her Shadow; the true ending is taking Wren's hand instead. *I am thou.*
  - **Result:** she goes home to all three worlds at once.
  - **Cost:** this world closes behind her and fades the way P3's memories of the Dark Hour do: S.E.E.S., the year, Akihiko. He keeps something small he can't explain.
  - **Requirements:** max bonds with a key person from each world plus Akihiko, every memory fragment, and Wren's link complete. The final choice only appears if all of that is true.

Because all three worlds lost her, every ending leaves someone without her. That's the weight of the choice.
