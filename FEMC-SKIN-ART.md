# Skin art: outfits, faces and HD poses

*Persona 3: Solstice · split out Nov 7 2026 (Kat's plan). Make this list first: everything here is what the Wardrobe, the Boutique, the fitting room, the home screen and the win screen show, so an outfit looks finished everywhere but in battle. Battle pieces wait in `FEMC-BATTLE-ART.md`, to do all together at the end.*

**Order for each outfit:** 1. pose sheet → 2. scene portraits → 3. HD poses (only what's still missing is listed; the 11 skin-series looks need only their HD poses). **Banner art** is at the bottom: make a banner once its outfits exist.

**What's here (Nov 7):** 13 pose sheets and 16 scene-portrait sheets (the 13 collab outfits; plus faces for Off-Day Hoodie, Beanie & Apron and Gekkoukan Uniform), 61 HD pose sets (Solar's 19 new outfits, 18 poses each; 42 sets of 6 victory poses for the others), 8 banner pictures.

**How:** open a new ChatGPT message, attach the files on the **Attach** line in that order (all in `Femc Mode Images/`, mostly `Place Prompt Attachments/`), paste the prompt, send. Save into **New Adds** under its **Save as** name (HD poses: a folder per outfit) and tell Claude. One picture per message.

**Sharpness:** no Photoshop upscaling needed any more. When everything's made, Claude upscales all the art in one go on the PC.

**Tip:** if ChatGPT blocks a pose, reword it (e.g. "relaxed standing pose, hand resting on hip" instead of "hands on hips"); that often gets it through.

## HD poses: how they work

One figure per image, tall and sharp. A set doesn't have to be complete: the game uses whichever poses are there.

**The prompt (one figure per image; change the bracketed parts):**
Attach: the outfit's pose sheet from `Current Skins/` (or `Pose - Solar sheet.png` for her school uniform), then `Solar.png`
Save as: `solar-hd-<pose>.png` (school uniform) or `skin-<look id>-hd-<pose>.png` (an outfit)
```
A single full-body character illustration, portrait orientation, 2:3, as large and sharp as possible: ONE figure only, head to toe, filling the height of the image with a little space above the head and below the feet, on a plain transparent background (or plain white). The SAME person as the attached images: same face, eyes and hair, in exactly the outfit of the FIRST attached image [outfit name]. Pose: [the pose, e.g. arms crossed, a small confident smile, weight on one leg]. Persona 3 Reload official art style: clean crisp lineart, flat cel shading with hard-edged shadows, high detail on the face and hair. No text, no background, no other people.
```

**The 18 poses** (put one in the prompt's brackets; the word before the colon is the file name):
`arms-crossed`: arms crossed, a small confident smile, weight on one leg · `thinking`: one finger on her chin, looking up, thinking · `wave`: a friendly wave, a bright smile · `peace`: a peace sign by her cheek, a wink · `hair`: one hand running through her hair · `shy`: hands clasped behind her back, a shy smile, looking down a little · `study`: reading an open book held in both hands · `ramen`: holding a bowl of ramen and chopsticks, delighted · `watering`: watering a small potted plant with a little watering can · `evoker`: raising the Evoker to her temple, eyes closed, calm · `stretch`: stretching both arms over her head mid-yawn · `phone`: looking down at her phone, smiling at a message · `cheer`: a fist pump, cheering · `jump`: a happy jump, both feet off the ground · `tease`: a relaxed standing pose, one hand resting on her hip, a teasing smile · `heart`: making a heart with both hands in front of her chest · `lookback`: looking back over her shoulder at the viewer · `sewing`: sewing a button on with a needle and thread, concentrating

Other fighters get six victory poses per outfit (for the win screen); each one's six are listed in its block.
