## Decisions for you

Each one has Claude's lean in brackets. None of them needs answering until the epilogue is built.

1. **How long she was gone.** One world's ending and your epilogue plan disagree about how much time passed back home. Option A: change about four lines of the ending to match your plan and the other worlds *(Claude's lean)*. Option B: the epilogue honours the ending, which changes that world's cover story.
2. **What the friends remember.** In the same world, a couple of lines in the ending blur what some of the searchers remember. Sharpen them to match your memory rules *(lean)*, or keep the blur and settle it in the epilogue's first scene?
3. **Runners: her parents.** Your plan has Thanksgiving break covering the absence. Some things already in the game make that harder than it sounds. Should the cover story also have to work on her parents *(lean: yes, and her father doesn't believe it, the way it is in strangers)*, or do her parents already know something's wrong?
4. **Searchers you can miss.** A few searchers can be missed in a playthrough (they wait for New Game+), and your plans give some of them big epilogue roles. Should they count as having crossed in every playthrough *(lean: yes for Equinox, plus one line of flavour in the epilogue if you never met them)*, or should the epilogue change depending on who you actually met? A few lines in the main game would also be needed so they're accounted for at the end.
5. **An earlier promise.** On one route, a character has already said something about the future that pulls against your planned final scene and Equinox's opening. Reframe the scene so it fits what was said *(lean: Claude thinks it can hit harder this way, without changing a word of the main game)*, or rethink that route's final scene?
6. **Small text.** The home endings each close with a quiet line about what Solar will or won't ever learn, and the glitches and Equinox will contradict it. Let the sequel break it on purpose *(lean)*, or soften a few words now? Also: OK to make one single-word fix so an ending matches "she remembers all three lives"?
7. **Equinox's opening.** Your shared final image has someone holding onto Solar in every world, and Equinox opens with all three. But on a route where she goes home to one world, she isn't in the other two. Should Equinox:
   - open on your route's moment only;
   - show all three as echoes; or
   - let the pull reach all three worlds even where she's missing, with someone holding onto the gap she left *(lean: this one, with your route's moment in focus)*?
8. **The two-world boys.** Haechan and Jungwon each live two lives, so in any route's epilogue one of those lives is in a world Solar didn't go back to. Let that other life show through them *(lean: lightly, as part of the dread)*, or keep it offstage?
9. **True ending, memory and shape.** Your lore bible says the true ending's world fades the way P3's Dark Hour memories do.
   - Does that fade reach Solar and the searchers too, or only the people of this world? *(lean: only this world's people, and Equinox gives it back)*
   - Should its epilogue braid all three worlds, follow one world you choose, or be a short three-part coda? *(lean: braid them if there's room, otherwise the coda)*
10. **Stay and bad.**
    - Stay: no epilogue, a short coda, or a "reverse" epilogue in Iwatodai where the signals come *from* the three worlds? *(lean: the reverse epilogue; it leads straight into Equinox)*
    - Bad: no epilogue, so it stays a P5R-style dead end *(lean)*, or a tiny teaser?
