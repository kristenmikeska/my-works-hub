## 5. Backgrounds: what each prompt asks for

| Kind of picture | Background | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Pose sheets (first and second), theme frames | **Transparent** | They get cut out. |
| Theme effect sheets (6.1) | **Solid black** | Glowing effects come out better keyed from black than with ChatGPT's transparency: soft glows turn muddy or get cut off. |
| Theurgy cut-ins, finish cards, entrance cards, One More strips, couple Showtimes | **Their own full background** | They fill the screen. |

If a transparent one comes back with a background anyway, that's fine: Claude cuts it out.
