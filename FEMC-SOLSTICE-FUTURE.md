# Persona 3: Solstice: future plans (DLC, epilogue, second game)

Kat's brainstorm doc. Ideas only, nothing is built. Spoiler-free about the main game's endings.

---

## Kat's concepts

**Concept 1: second game.**
- This time *all* the characters from the three universes wake up in the Personaverse, or appear gradually, the way they do now. Solar's parents from every universe might come too.
- Nobody knows whether they can get back to their worlds. Everyone has to weigh staying in the Personaverse against going home, and each choice has its own pros and cons.
- The game's central question: why have they all been brought back?

**Concept 2: Solstice DLC, or an epilogue act.**
- Four new stories. Depending on the route, the player follows Solar's life back home: Hoax, Runners or Strangers, each drawing on that novel's themes.
- Alternative: an **epilogue act after March** instead of a DLC. While she lives her world's story, Solar starts noticing signs of the Personaverse: glitches, signals, and hints that her Persona is still inside her. This would lead into the second game.

**Concept 3: Runners' future canon.**
- In Runners' planned future, Solar goes to Korea, becomes an idol and joins LE SSERAFIM. That makes her soul sisters with Yunjin in Runners too, just later on.
- It brings in more characters: Chaewon, Kazuha, Sakura and Eunchae.
- The risk is that it gets messy or confusing.

## Claude's take

**These three connect into one arc:**
1. **Solstice, plus an epilogue act** (Concept 2 as an epilogue, not a DLC).
   - This is the P3 FES "The Answer" idea, but better, because each route gets its own epilogue in its own world.
   - The glitches and Persona hints at the end give the player a reason to come back.
   - Because it's part of the same game, saves carry over naturally.
2. **Solstice 2** (Concept 1).
   - The signals in the epilogue pay off when *everyone* wakes up in the Personaverse.
   - "Should I stay?" becomes the core theme, for every character and not just Solar. That's a strong central question.
   - Bringing back the parents is a great source of tension. Their Personaverse versions could finally meet the S.E.E.S. world.

**Concept 3 fits best inside the Runners epilogue,** as a flash-forward or its closing chapters rather than a separate thing.
- Solar's path toward LE SSERAFIM is her Runners future.
- Yunjin's "we're soul sisters in every world" pays off on a route that isn't hers, which could be the answer to the Yunjin heartache.
- Chaewon, Kazuha, Sakura and Eunchae come in naturally there.
- In Solstice 2 they could be among the people who wake up.
- It only gets messy if it's dropped into the Personaverse year itself. Keep it in Runners' future.

**Kat's answers (2026-10-25):**
- **Epilogue length:** a few weeks per route as the middle ground, so there's a substantial story and the subtle psychological horror and tension don't drag. It could stretch to a month if there's enough content. There's lots of room for romance in each world.
- **How the epilogue plays:** still to be decided once the plan is more thorough. It could work either way.
  - If it plays like the main game, the battles are part of the glitches.
  - The signs start very small, and battles only come much later, near the end. Solar gets half pulled into Tartarus fights, only partly there and glitching between the worlds.
  - Options for S.E.E.S.: they see her; or only Solar knows; or they can't see her at all, but she's somehow helping them fight.
- **Solstice 2 protagonist:** Solar.
- **Memory in Solstice 2:** everyone remembers each other. That's the most fun.
- **Parents in Solstice 2:** probably links.
- **The LE SSERAFIM thread:** probably teased on every route, so the new characters work in Solstice 2.

**Still open:**
- the epilogue's format (the full calendar, or story with glitch fights)
- S.E.E.S.'s side of the half-present battles
- why everyone is brought back in Solstice 2
- how each route's epilogue teases LE SSERAFIM without breaking that world's canon
