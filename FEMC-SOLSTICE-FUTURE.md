# Persona 3: Solstice: future plans (DLC, epilogue, second game)

Kat's brainstorm doc. Ideas only, nothing is built. Spoiler-free about the main game's endings.

---

## Kat's concepts

**Concept 1: second game.**
- This time *all* the characters from the three universes wake up in the Personaverse, or appear gradually, the way they do now. Solar's parents from every universe might come too.
- Nobody knows whether they can get back to their worlds. Everyone has to weigh staying in the Personaverse against going home, and each choice has its own pros and cons.
- The game's central question: why have they all been brought back?

**Concept 2: Solstice DLC, or an epilogue act.**
- Four new stories. Depending on the route, the player follows Solar's life back home: Hoax, Runners or Strangers, each drawing on that novel's themes.
- Alternative: an **epilogue act after March** instead of a DLC. While she lives her world's story, Solar starts noticing signs of the Personaverse: glitches, signals, and hints that her Persona is still inside her. This would lead into the second game.

**Concept 3: Runners' future canon.**
- In Runners' planned future, Solar goes to Korea, becomes an idol and joins LE SSERAFIM. That makes her soul sisters with Yunjin in Runners too, just later on.
- It brings in more characters: Chaewon, Kazuha, Sakura and Eunchae.
- The risk is that it gets messy or confusing.

---

## Claude's take

**These three connect into one arc:**
1. **Solstice, plus an epilogue act** (Concept 2 as an epilogue, not a DLC).
   - This is the P3 FES "The Answer" idea, but better, because each route gets its own epilogue in its own world.
   - The glitches and Persona hints at the end give the player a reason to come back.
   - Because it's part of the same game, saves carry over naturally.
2. **Solstice 2** (Concept 1).
   - The signals in the epilogue pay off when *everyone* wakes up in the Personaverse.
   - "Should I stay?" becomes the core theme, for every character and not just Solar. That's a strong central question.
   - Bringing back the parents is a great source of tension. Their Personaverse versions could finally meet the S.E.E.S. world.

**Concept 3 fits best inside the Runners epilogue,** as a flash-forward or its closing chapters rather than a separate thing.
- Solar's path toward LE SSERAFIM is her Runners future.
- Yunjin's "we're soul sisters in every world" pays off on a route that isn't hers, which could be the answer to the Yunjin heartache.
- Chaewon, Kazuha, Sakura and Eunchae come in naturally there.
- In Solstice 2 they could be among the people who wake up.
- It only gets messy if it's dropped into the Personaverse year itself. Keep it in Runners' future.

**Kat's answers (2026-10-25):**
- **Epilogue length:** a few weeks per route as the middle ground, so there's a substantial story and the subtle psychological horror and tension don't drag. It could stretch to a month if there's enough content. There's lots of room for romance in each world.
- **How the epilogue plays:** still to be decided once the plan is more thorough. It could work either way.
  - If it plays like the main game, the battles are part of the glitches.
  - The signs start very small, and battles only come much later, near the end. Solar gets half pulled into Tartarus fights, only partly there and glitching between the worlds.
  - Options for S.E.E.S.: they see her; or only Solar knows; or they can't see her at all, but she's somehow helping them fight.
- **Solstice 2 protagonist:** Solar.
- **Memory in Solstice 2:** everyone remembers each other. That's the most fun.
- **Parents in Solstice 2:** probably links.
- **The LE SSERAFIM thread:** probably teased on every route, so the new characters work in Solstice 2.

**Still open:**
- the epilogue's format (the full calendar, or story with glitch fights)
- S.E.E.S.'s side of the half-present battles
- why everyone is brought back in Solstice 2
- how each route's epilogue teases LE SSERAFIM without breaking that world's canon

## Epilogue planning: Kat's notes, 2026-10-26

**Branching inside each world.** Each epilogue has a few routes. The player can experience different stories, or the epilogue's plot changes depending on their choices.

**Cover stories.** In each world, Solar has been missing for a while, and so have the friends who went looking for her. Each world has its own explanation.
- **Runners:** a campus rumor says certain Sigma Chi and Alpha Phi members went on *another* joint trip, one so "exclusive" that not even the whole chapter was invited. (Students already think the chapters get special treatment, because they kind of do.) Everyone goes along with the rumor and gets thrown back into Syracuse life.
  - This picks up around late November in canon, after the camping trip.
  - If it follows canon exactly, the Mark/Solar fallout has happened, which might need small edits to the main story. The game already makes Mark feel a bit estranged from everyone, which helps. Kat says we don't always have to follow canon exactly, but it's good drama.
  - Tap back into what's happening in canon then: eSports and so on.
  - A Sigma party, for sure.
- **strangers and HOAX:** not fleshed out yet. To work out together.

**The glitches.** Open question: does Haechan (or Jungwon in HOAX) notice? Do others see them later, or only Solar?
- Kat envisions a scene, possibly at the very end, where Solar is being pulled between two universes and Haechan is trying to save her. She's panicking, it's deeply emotional, and he promises he'll come find her, whatever universe she ends up in.

**The pull-back reason:** a mix of "the bridge never closed" and "someone is doing it on purpose."

**Name:** *Persona 3: Equinox* for the sequel. Kat likes it.

**LE SSERAFIM:** the girls start showing up as small side characters in each world. In strangers, Yunjin is Solar's age and already a member, so they're peers there, not kids.

**Carry-over from the epilogue ending:** unsure. Kat doesn't want Solstice 2 to change depending on the ending.

## Claude's suggestions on these (for discussion)

**Cover stories, starting points:**
- **strangers:** the Lee house buries it. The official line is that Solar was sent abroad (or is "visiting Dalian"). Haechan and Jungwon vanishing at the same time makes each house suspect the other, so the epilogue opens with the families on edge.
- **HOAX:** the president and the vice president both go missing. It's covered as a council retreat or a suspension, the school forum invents reasons, and there's an impeachment rumor. Jihoon would enjoy all of it far too much.

**Who notices the glitches:**
- Solar always notices first.
- Next is the person who was in the Personaverse with her and is her constant in that world: Haechan in Runners and strangers, Jungwon in HOAX. They notice later and more faintly, and dismiss it until they can't.
- Others only see what's left behind: a stopped clock, a blue butterfly.

**The final scene:**
- **Runners and strangers:** Haechan holding onto her as she's pulled. "I'll find you. Every world." It's the epilogue's last scene, and Equinox opens on him keeping that promise.
- **HOAX** is the world without Haechan. There, Jungwon holds on, and Haechan's voice comes through the bleed, from somewhere else. That's devastating in the right way.

**Mixing the two reasons:** the bridge never closed, and someone found it and is widening it on purpose. The epilogue's glitches are the bridge fraying. Equinox reveals that someone is pulling.

**Carry-over:** Equinox opens the same way whatever happened. The epilogue's route only adds flavour: Solar's first memory and a few lines. That way nothing branches into a mess.

**Mark:** before planning, check how much Mark's estrangement already shows in the game. The epilogue can open mid-fallout, as if the Personaverse year were a pause, so the main game probably needs only a few small Mark lines, not a rewrite.

**Kat, 2026-10-26:**
- The strangers and HOAX cover stories above are approved.
- In strangers, both Haechan and Jungwon notice the glitches, since both are in that world.

## Epilogue planning, round 2 (2026-10-26)

**Memory rules (proposed).**
- Solar is one person living three lives (lore bible), so there are never multiple Solars.
- In the epilogue she remembers all three lives and the Personaverse. That's why HOAX's gap hurts.
- Haechan and Jungwon remember too, in the worlds where they exist.
- Claude will check this privately against the endings' logic so nothing contradicts them, without spoiling Kat.

**Runners (Kat's canon timeline):**
- 11/16: camping ends.
- 11/19: Haechan tells Solar the whole truth.
- 11/21: Sigma party; Solar and the girls have a girls' night instead.
- 11/22: Solar goes home for Thanksgiving.
- 12/1: she's back. Chenle walks in on Haechan's track with her stem.
- 12/2–3: Haechan tells her about the track and offers to delete it; she says don't.
- 12/6–7: Regionals. Minji's closure talk. Haechan starts to fit in with her friends.
- 12/9–10: the studio, on her terms.
- 12/11–14: they turn toward Danielle and settle into the social world as a couple.
- 12/19: Sigma party. Danielle gets footage of how close they really are.

**Proposed Runners epilogue:**
- Pick up in early December.
- The absence overlaps Thanksgiving break, which covers it naturally. Chenle, Minji and Jaehyun (also in the Personaverse) were gone for less time, so they all need to keep the story straight.
- Danielle was in the Personaverse too, so she knows the story is fake. She's the threat. Mark stays complicated, not a villain.
- The 12/19 footage shows something impossible: Solar flickering. That's the climax, and possibly the "I'll find you" scene.
- Routes: romance, with Hanni and Hyein defending the emergency vacation.

**HOAX:**
- The council retreat is the cover, since the council were in the Personaverse too. Solar's friend group, being less popular, mostly went unnoticed.
- The impeachment drama is approved.
- Glitches start funny-eerie and turn unsettling.

**strangers:**
- The truce is likely cracked by Ch 65. Claude will check privately; no spoilers for Kat.

**Tone:**
- strangers: the most dangerous, with intense violence.
- Runners: the most romantic and physical, with substance abuse and fist fights.
- HOAX: goofy slice-of-life romance with its own drama, plus substance abuse and fist fights.

## Epilogue planning, round 3 (2026-10-26)

**strangers (revised after a private canon check, spoiler-free):**
- It's deep winter and mid-crisis.
- The two houses aren't each other's main problem anymore; the pressure comes from outside.
- **The cover:** her father hides her absence so the house doesn't look weak to its enemies.
- **Her father:** he knows she's lying and chooses not to push yet.
- **Haechan and Jungwon:** both remember, both see the glitches, and share the secret.
- **The glitches carry real consequences:** a gun that won't fire, the tide stopping, a call that reaches Iwatodai for a second.
- **Routes:** Haechan, Jungwon, or family.
- **Open:** follow canon's events closely, or run alongside them?

**HOAX (deeper):**
- The council returns to a forum full of theories and an impeachment motion.
- Jihoon pretends the Personaverse didn't happen.
- Solar keeps reaching for a name, and Jungwon notices.
- **The glitches escalate:** comedy (the haunted vending machine, a Hate Club séance), then unsettling (footage, the notes), then real (the gym at midnight).
- **Routes:** Jungwon, Jihoon's redemption, the council.

**Shared ending image:** in every world, someone holds onto Solar as she's pulled.
- Runners: Haechan.
- strangers: Haechan and Jungwon together.
- HOAX: Jungwon, with Haechan's voice coming through.

Equinox opens with all three moments from the Personaverse's side.
