# Persona 3: Equinox · cast ideas

Ideas for who comes back and how, so the links don't feel like Solstice again. No story here. Pick, mix or cross things out.

---

## A. Keeping returning links fresh

Everyone remembers Solstice. Doing the same links again would feel like a rerun. Here are seven ways to handle it.

**1. Second arcs.** The link picks up where Solstice left off. New ranks, a new problem, a new place. It's the same person, a year older.
- Good: it rewards people who played Solstice. Characters grow.
- Bad: it's a lot of writing. A new player starts in the middle of someone's story.

**2. A new theme for every link.** Each link is about one question this year: is this person staying or going home, and why? Every rank pushes on that.
- Good: it ties every link to the game's big question. Nobody repeats their Solstice ranks.
- Bad: if it's overdone, every link sounds the same.

**3. Duo and group links.** Two or more people share one link, and the fun is in how they bounce off each other. Mixing worlds works best, like a Persona 3 character with a novel character.
- Good: it's fresh, it's funny, and it means fewer links overall. The worlds get to meet properly.
- Bad: there's less one-on-one time. It doesn't suit romances.

**4. A new arcana.** A returning link gets a new tarot card that shows how they've changed. The old card sits beside it on the Social Link screen.
- Good: you can see the change at a glance. It's a nice Persona touch.
- Bad: it needs new card art.

**5. Side stories instead of links.** A short chain of 3 to 5 scenes, like a small side quest, with a small reward. No ranks and no perks.
- Good: it's light. Lots of people can have a little moment.
- Bad: they're less deep, and there are no perks.

**6. Cameos.** People are around town, at school, at dinner, in group chats and on the phone. They have no scenes of their own.
- Good: the world feels full. There's almost nothing to build.
- Bad: fans of that character may want more.

**7. Retire a few.** Some people come back only as cameos because their story feels finished.

**My recommendation: mix them.**
- Canon romances and the people closest to Solar get **second arcs** (1), all with the **new theme** (2). Some get a **new card** (4).
- A lot of the rest become **duo or group links** across worlds (3). This is the freshest part.
- Persona 3's wider cast gets **side stories** (5) and **cameos** (6).
- A few links are **retired** to cameos (7).
- That makes about 33 links (Solstice had 39), with far more people on screen.

## C. Persona 3 notes

- **Shinjiro:** yes. In Solstice he wakes in late February, at the end of his link. In Equinox he's awake from day one for everyone, whatever happened in your Solstice game. He's still getting his strength back early on.
- **The P3 school and town links** were background in Solstice. Equinox can finally give them a little story each, as side stories, so the town feels lived in without 15 more full links.
- **Leaving out Strega, Ryoji and Ikutsuki** keeps Persona 3's own ending untouched.

## D. LE SSERAFIM: "one version or all of them?"

Here's what I meant, in plain terms.

- In the epilogues, the four girls show up in every world, but as different people. In Runners, Sakura is a caster at Regionals and Eunchae is on a campus tour. In strangers, they're Yunjin's dance-studio friends. In HOAX, Kazuha and Eunchae go to Hanlim.
- So Sakura (for example) exists three times, once in each world, as three different girls.
- When she wakes up in the Personaverse, which Sakura is she?

**Option 1: one version each.** Each girl comes from one world only (say, Sakura from Runners, Kazuha from HOAX). She only knows that world's Solar.
- Simple, but each girl only fits with one world's crowd.

**Option 2: all her versions in one.** Each girl is one person who remembers all of her lives, like Haechan and Jungwon remember two. Sakura remembers being the caster, the dance friend and whoever she was in HOAX.
- More fun: "which one of me are you talking to?" She can be friends with everyone, and it fits "every universe".

**My recommendation: Option 2.** Same rule as the two-world boys. In private with Solar they talk about all their lives. With everyone else it just shows in habits.

**Yunjin** stays the Yunjin from strangers that you know from Solstice. She already has a whole history with Solar, and the other four can meet her as herself.

## E. Romance: things to talk through

Once the cast is settled:
1. Do the canon romances stay the same: Haechan, Jungwon, Jihoon, Minji, Akihiko and Shinjiro?
2. Should anyone new be a canon romance? Or a side romance (for example Junpei, as in P3P, or someone from the P3 cast)?
3. Every romance starts over, but everyone remembers what they were to Solar. How much should Solstice's romance show up in the first weeks?
4. The same rules as Solstice for dating more than one person (honest and torn, no cheating until both routes are chosen)?
5. With the parents in town, how much do they react to who Solar's dating? Three dads at once?
6. The Akihiko and Haechan rivalry: pick up where Solstice left it, or start a new stage?
7. Jungwon is her boyfriend in HOAX and her fiancé in strangers. How should Equinox handle both at once?
8. Jihoon's HOAX scandal: keep that thread going here?
9. Should any romance be able to end with someone staying in the Personaverse with her, or going home with her?
10. The same spice level and rules as Solstice?
