# DO NOT OPEN (Kat): FEMC main story plan, full spoilers

Kat asked to experience the story for the first time by playing it. This file is for Claude and any agent writing story events. Never quote it to Kat or summarize plot in chat.

## Shape

Four acts across the calendar (4/7/2009 → 1/31/2010), an original mystery with Persona 3 as the backdrop. Every P3 full moon (5/9, 6/8, 7/7, 8/6, 9/5, 10/4, 11/3, and on) brings a new wave of visitors from other worlds; that's already established in Elizabeth's arrival texts. Story events live in `assets/femc/story.js` (format at the top of that file) and play on their own on Solar's social links page when their date arrives.

Theme (Kat's own motifs): the road not taken, counter-lives, endings people run from. Every visitor is running from an ending in their own story (Noctis's crown, Widowmaker's chair, Astarion's master, Leon's next mission, Cloud's lost memories, Kasumi's grief, Judy's Evelyn). The antagonist offers them all the same thing: stay, and never have to reach it.

## Key facts (canon for the story)

- **Visitors don't become coffins.** They come from outside this world, so they stay awake in the Dark Hour. That's how S.E.E.S. first notices them.
- **Bonds anchor them.** Elizabeth: unbonded visitors slowly fade into the hour. Each social link is literally keeping someone here safely until they can go home.
- **Wren** (original character): a girl from a world of endless railways (a quiet, rainy, steam-and-signal-lamp world), about Solar's age, in a station porter's coat too big for her. She arrived in April and keeps "missing" the last train because she's afraid of where it goes. Solar meets her on the Iwatodai platform in Act I. She has no social link (she can't hold one; her page in the compendium is faint from the start). She's the story's heart: the everyvisitor.
- **The Lacuna** (antagonist, a Shadow): born in the gap between worlds from every ending the visitors refuse. It looks like a figure made of blank pages, faceless, with a voice that sounds like whoever is listening wants it to. Its offer: stay in the green hour forever, in a world where nothing ever has to end. It has been tearing pages out of Elizabeth's compendium: visitors it has "kept." It is not cruel; it genuinely believes it is being kind (a dark mirror of Maruki, which Kasumi's link can echo).
- **Elizabeth** knows more than she says early on, and admits in Act II that her compendium is also a door.
- **S.E.E.S.** (Mitsuru, Akihiko, Yukari, Junpei, later Fuuka, Aigis, Ken, Koromaru, Shinjiro until 10/4) are the frame. P3's own plot (Strega, the twelve Shadows, Nyx) stays offstage, felt only as "the operation" on full moons. Don't contradict or spoil P3 canon; don't rewrite 10/4.

## Act I · Arrivals (4/7 → 6/8)

1. **4/9 evening · Iwatodai Dorm · Lounge · "people who don't sleep"**: Mitsuru, Akihiko, Yukari, Junpei at the lounge. Mitsuru mentions reports from the Kirijo Group's sensors: figures moving during the Dark Hour who aren't coffins and aren't Shadows. Junpei jokes about ghosts. Solar knows more than she can say (Elizabeth's secret from the intro); choice to hint, deflect, or stay quiet. Ends with Solar looking out the window at midnight and seeing, far down the street, someone standing in the green light, awake.
2. **4/16 evening · Iwatodai Station · Platform · "the last train"**: Solar meets Wren at the station after the last train has gone, sitting on a bench with a ticket she never uses. Lovely, strange, a little funny. Wren says the trains here are "too quiet." She asks Solar what happens to people who stay somewhere they don't belong. The Dark Hour starts; Wren doesn't become a coffin. She sees Solar doesn't either. "Oh. You too?"
3. **5/9 night · "the first full moon"**: the operation (offstage, P3's monorail). Afterward, on the walk home, Solar sees the city full of awake strangers for the first time: a girl in a red jacket running, a fox made of light on the shrine steps, a woman in a white coat helping someone up. (No names; links introduce them.) Elizabeth's voice in her head: "More guests."
4. **5/23 evening · The Velvet Room · "the faint page"** (theme velvet): Elizabeth shows Solar the compendium: Wren's page is faint, half-blank. "Pages fade when no one holds them." And another page, torn out cleanly. Elizabeth did not tear it. First hint someone else is reading the book.
5. **6/8 night · Iwatodai Station · Platform · The Dark Hour · "the blank page"** (Act I finale): after the operation, Solar finds Wren on the platform in the green hour, talking to someone made of pages. The Lacuna speaks for the first time (soft, kind, horrible). It offers Wren a train that never has to arrive. Solar interrupts; it turns its blank face to her, says "You'll understand. Everyone does, eventually," and comes apart into loose paper in the wind. Wren, shaken, holds Solar's sleeve. End on Elizabeth, later: "Someone has been reading my book."

## Act II · Summer of Strangers (6/9 → 8/31)

The visitors start finding each other; S.E.E.S. learns about them. Events (to write): a dorm dinner where whichever visitors Solar has met show up (`ifRank` cameos); Mitsuru confronting Solar about the awake people; Wren getting a little more solid every time Solar visits (a summer day at the beach or the station); the Lacuna taking Wren into the Dark Hour on the 7/7 or 8/6 full moon; S.E.E.S. plus every visitor Solar has bonded with going into Tartarus after her (finale on 8/6, each met visitor gets one line or one moment; unmet ones stay offstage). Elizabeth's confession: the compendium is a door, and the Lacuna found it.

## Act III · The Thinning (9/1 → 11/30)

The Dark Hour leaks into daylight: coffins at noon for a heartbeat, visitors flickering. The Lacuna makes its offer to visitors Solar knows, timed alongside their own links' darkest ranks (Noctis "what's coming", Widowmaker "talon", Astarion "ascension", Cloud "the voice", Kasumi "me"); story events can reference them with `ifRank`. 10/4 is Shinjiro's (respect it; at most a quiet beat on 10/5). Finale on 11/3: the Lacuna makes Solar her own offer, showing her pages of her other lives (Runners, Strangers, Hoax, at blurb level only, like Rohan's rank 3), and a world where she never has to choose between any of them or lose anyone in this one.

## Act IV · The Last Pages (12/1 → 1/31)

Gathering every bond. Wren's choice (she gets on the train). The visitors deciding to go home to their endings, because the bond goes with them. Ending: built last, separately; don't write it until Kat asks.

### Act II as written (keep later acts consistent with this)

Events in `story.js`: `a2-hole-in-the-data` 6/17, `a2-hot-pot` 7/18, `a2-end-of-the-line` 7/30, `a2-the-door` 8/1, `a2-no-trains` 8/6, `a2-the-kept` 8/7. `femcMetAny(ids, then, else)` at the top of story.js gives "met any of these" branches.

- **6/17 Command Room**: Fuuka's Lucia hears the visitors "like radios tuned to far stations" (32 of them; one "sounds like a bell", Kiriko's fox). Mitsuru's sensor trace of 6/8 shows Solar, Wren as a faint flicker, and the Lacuna as a clean gap ("a hole in the data"). Solar tells some truth; Solar keeps Wren's name to herself. Mitsuru decides **not to tell the Kirijo Group yet** and says "bring them here; I'd rather know their faces than their readings."
- **7/18 hot pot** (day after exams): met visitors turn up (Kuromi's toll, Haru takes over Fuuka's broth, Ann learns the S.E.E.S. name, Kiriko hides Solar's fortune in her sleeve without saying what it was, Mercy rewraps Junpei's wrist, Astarion must be invited in by Mitsuru, Aphrodite blesses Junpei, Cloud found the dorm because Wren told him). Wren won't come in ("I'd like to be a bit more of a person first"); she stands under the April streetlight and casts a shadow for the first time.
- **7/30 Seaside Park**: Wren's first daylight, first train ride that "arrived exactly where it said it would", first sea. Her world's lore: the line never reaches the sea; the last train goes to the coast and nobody comes back (her grandmother). Her footprints stay in the sand.
- **8/1 Velvet Room**: Wren's page is dark and full again, with a small figure of Solar drawn beside her on the bench. Three torn stubs now. Elizabeth's confession: every page is a **door** (how guests arrive and how they'll go home); the thing comes in at the gutter; it got in because Elizabeth sat up reading with the book open ("something began to read back"). Closing the book would strand everyone. It takes faint pages easily; a page worth keeping is worth taking whole.
- **8/6**: after the operation Wren's bench is empty, her ticket punched. A silent black-green train with paper-lantern windows, full of sleepers, takes her (she "asked" where it goes; "nowhere"). The rails lift into the sky toward Tartarus. Wren's page is gone; the gutter shows a line of light (door left open). Elizabeth notes new guests arrived that night (Noctis, Judy, Shadowheart; unnamed).
- **8/7 finale**: met visitors come to the dorm unasked, having felt "a tug under the ribs" (the bonds). Elizabeth physically meets them in Tartarus and opens the stub as a door to **the Platform Between**: an endless station with blank timetables and one looping train of **the kept**, sleeping visitors from many worlds, peaceful. Each met visitor gets a moment (Sebastian: "a screensaver"; Kiriko's fox finds the fourth car; Cloud cuts the coupling, else Aigis shoots the wheels; Ann's whip; Aphrodite pushes back the green; Kuromi; Haru "I know that face"; Astarion refuses its whispered offer; Mercy takes Wren's pulse at dawn). Wren gets off by her own choice. The Lacuna lets her go: "I've already given her what she asked for. You can't hold all of them. The hour is only so wide. For now."
- **Wren is changed**: solid, warm, but her ticket's destination is blank and she can't remember where her train goes or what she was afraid of. Her ending was taken.
- **Named**: Elizabeth names it **the Lacuna** ("the place in an old text where something has been lost"): it is no longer keeping visitors, it is **taking the endings out of them**. Its speaker id is still `lacuna` shown as '???'; later acts can add a cast id such as `thelacuna: 'The Lacuna'` once Solar uses the name.
- **Act III hook**: at dawn on 8/8 Solar sees a salaryman become a coffin in full sunlight for one blink ("She'd deal with it in September").
- Not used in Act II: Shinjiro (his link brings him back 8/7 on its own), Noctis/Judy/Shadowheart (arrive 8/6, only glimpsed as "more guests"), Leon/Rohan/Kasumi/Widowmaker/Haechan. Rio appears only as a text at dawn (she knows nothing about the hour). Aigis, Ken and Koromaru are with S.E.E.S. by the finale; Fuuka has no portrait (speaks without `f`).
