# Late-game skill branches: the design sheet (draft for Kat)

Roadmap section 4, "late-game branches so nobody maxes out early". Kat picked ideas 1, 4, 5 and 6 on Oct 31 2026, with idea 2 underneath everything. This sheet shapes them so she can sign off before anything is built. Spoiler-free: names and mechanics only.

## Why it's needed
- **Solar** earns 2 points a level plus boss and memory points, so she has well over 200 by level 99. Haenim's sun costs about 200, so she can finish it before the end.
- **The party** gets about 55-60 points by level 99, against about 85 for a whole orbit, so they never max out.
- **New Game+ already keeps the battle save:** levels, skill points and owned nodes carry into the next run. So points keep piling up in the New Game+ climb (255F-400F), with nothing new to spend them on.

**Recommendation on New Game+:** keep it as it is. Everyone keeps their levels and points. That's how Persona 3 Reload does it, and it's what gives the climb something to pay off.

## The four pieces

### 1. The Eclipse ring (Solar, New Game+)
- **What:** a new ring outside Haenim's sun, one segment per ray (Striker, Trickster, Mage, Healer, Guard, Support), plus one crown at the very top.
- **Opening:** the New Game+ climb. The first climb block's summit opens two segments, the second opens two more, the third opens the last two, and beating floor 400's final boss opens the crown.
- **Each segment:** three nodes (3, 4 and 5 points), then a **Solstice** capstone (8 points). The capstone is a stronger version of that ray's keystone or signature skill.
- **The crown:** *Total Eclipse* (15 points). Once per battle, Solar's next skill also counts as a hit of every element the target is weak to.
- **Total:** about 135 points, which is roughly what a long New Game+ climb hands out.
- **Look:** a dark ring with a pink rim around the sun. Locked segments show as an eclipse shadow until they open.

### 2. Radiance (everyone, the safety net)
- **What:** once every node on a ray is owned (including its Eclipse segment for Solar), a **Radiance** node appears at the end of that ray.
- **How it works:** it can be bought again and again, at 2 points each time, up to 10 times. Each buy gives a small bonus on that ray's theme, such as +1% crit on Striker, +2% Fire/Ice/Elec on Mage, or +1% healing on Healer.
- **Caps:** the existing passive caps still apply, so Radiance can never break balance; it just stops counting at the cap.
- **For the party:** same idea, one Radiance node per build once that build is full.
- **Result:** spare points are never wasted, but nothing gets out of hand.

### 3. The epilogue branches (Solar, after each world)
Finishing an epilogue world opens a small branch on Haenim's sun, between two rays. Each branch has five nodes at 2 points each: three passives and two actives. Every node is named after its world.

- **Runners**, between Trickster and Striker:
  - *Clutch Play*: stronger when she's the last one standing.
  - *Respawn*: once per battle, she gets back up at a little HP.
  - *Overtime*: buffs last one turn longer in long fights.
  - *Team Comms*: the party's first action each battle is quicker.
  - *Grandmaster*: an active. A precise single hit that can't miss.
- **Strangers**, between Mage and Guard:
  - *Undertow*: Ice hits slow the target.
  - *Low Tide*: lower damage taken while guarding.
  - *Red Crane*: an active. Lifts a Shadow out of the fight for one turn.
  - *Daeboreum Moon*: Dark resistance, and a small heal under a full moon.
  - *Nightwalk*: better first strikes in Tartarus.
- **HOAX**, between Support and Healer:
  - *Point of Order*: cancels a Shadow's buff when she lands a weakness.
  - *Rebuttal*: a counter chance after being debuffed.
  - *Motion Carried*: an active. Party-wide Tarukaja and Rakukaja.
  - *Recess*: a small SP refund each turn.
  - *Landslide*: an active. All-target damage that grows with the number of foes.
- **Stay**, between Healer and Guard:
  - *Hanami*: a small party regen.
  - *Petal Shield*: the first hit each battle on a party member is reduced.
  - *Promise*: links with S.E.E.S. members give a little extra stat bonus.
  - *Spring Again*: an active. Revives one ally at full HP, once per battle.
  - *Stay*: her guard also covers whoever's next to her.

## Balance rules (unchanged from the party trees)
- **Solar is always the strongest.** Party upgrades make *her* better; they don't make the party stronger than her.
- **The caps still hold.** Crit, evasion, counter and follow-up caps stay where they are, so no chain can run away.
- **New Game+ difficulty:** the climb's Shadows are set by floor, not by level, so the extra power is earned against harder floors.
- **Tests:** a maxed late-game party still deals less than Solar on a weakness fight, and the existing balance test is extended to cover it.
