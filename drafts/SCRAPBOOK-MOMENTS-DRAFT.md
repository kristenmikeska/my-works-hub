## 1. Runners (Syracuse, through Ch 66)

1. ★ **Not Harvard** · Ch 1 "where it begins"
   *Solar Lee turned down Harvard and climbed out of her father's car at Syracuse, telling herself this time she'd live for herself.*
   Why: sets up the Lee family (Lee Industries, Upper East Side), her escape motive, and that Jaehyun is already at SU.

2. **The boy who already knew her name** · Ch 2 "the biggest opportunity"
   *Day one, and Mark Lee was already walking her through the Extracurricular Building. He's the one who pointed out the brand-new eSports lab.*
   Why: introduces Mark as the easy, safe friend from the very first day, and the triangle's starting point.

3. ★ **Grandmaster 3** · Ch 4 "grandmaster 3"
   *She walked into eSports tryouts as a Kiriko main, rank Grandmaster 3, and walked out with a spot on the team.*
   Why: establishes her gaming life, the Overwatch team, and the identity that's hers alone, not the family's.

4. **The Lee siblings** · Ch 15 "the lee siblings"
   *Chanel dress, press line, her father's bodyguards. For one afternoon she was a Lee again, smiling for cameras while her big brother got the trophy.*
   Why: shows the public Lee machine, her father, and Jaehyun as the family's heir and Sigma Chi president (his presidency is named in Ch 2).

5. ★ **"This fits you better than that"** · Ch 16 "performing never ends"
   *Out of the fancy dress, in a jersey too big for her and stars on her cheeks. Haechan looked her over on the bleachers and said it fit her better.*
   Why: the first real Solar and Haechan moment, where he sees past the Lee persona. The line comes back in Ch 62.

6. **The rooftop** · Ch 33 to Ch 37 ("a personal invite" through "no coming back")
   *On the Language Arts rooftop he didn't ask for her father's connections. He asked her to sing someday. In Ch 37 he played her his unfinished track.*
   Why: Haechan's music, the stolen-demo backstory with Mark, and the night they first sleep together.

7. **The living-room meeting** · Ch 39 "the escape of shame"
   *Jaehyun came home, called everyone into the Sigma living room, and drew a line through both boys. Haechan sent her away cold.*
   Why: Jaehyun's control over her life, and the first rupture that starts Haechan's relapse arc.

8. ★ **MVP** · Ch 45 "frequency"
   *They won the collegiate invitational and Solar was match MVP. Minji says she called it. Somewhere in the back, someone else was watching too.*
   Why: her biggest win and Minji as her duo and best friend. Haechan watches unseen, and Jaehyun quietly protects eSports from their father.

9. ★ **Halloween hallway** · Ch 51 "the decision" and Ch 52 "remember that"
   *Hanni and Hyein staged an intervention, Minji opened the door, and Solar finally said his real name out loud: Donghyuck.*
   Why: Hanni and Hyein as her sorority sisters and protectors, his feelings confirmed as real (since August), and the start of the secret period.

10. ★ **Full moon in the valley** · Ch 62 "next time"
    *2 AM, the coldest night of the year. A leaning tree, an ugly blanket, a bag of gas-station snacks, and his jacket: "it fits you better." Then, "next time."*
    Why: the softest moment in the book and the state of their secret, undefined relationship. Her confirming the Lee travel here also plants the November family obligations.

11. **Saturday with Mark** · Ch 66 "suns in november"
    *November 8, a whole day out with Mark. Gold suns on a scarf. She liked it more than she expected, and felt guilty about that, and then irritated that she felt guilty.*
    Why: where the book stops. She's still split between the two boys, still suspicious of Mark, and the camping trip with no cell service is coming.

*Cast note for captions:* Hanni and Hyein (Alpha Phi sisters), Minji (eSports duo), Jaehyun (brother, Sigma Chi president), Mark and Haechan (Sigma brothers, ex-best friends).

## 2. strangers (Incheon 2012–13, through Ch 65)

1. ★ **3:07 a.m., the back gate** · Ch 1 "low tide"
   *The restaurant daughter has a secret: she stitches her father's men in the laundry room. Tonight it's Haechan, with nineteen stitches and a joke for every one.*
   Why: establishes Solar as Lee Taejoon's daughter (Haesong, the harbor), her five years of secret stitching, and Haechan.

2. ★ **"Whether either of us is still alive"** · Ch 2 "funerals and weddings"
   *A banquet for twenty and the funeral-and-wedding soju. Her father's oldest rival raised a toast: Solar Lee will marry Yang Jungwon on April 13th.*
   Why: the arranged engagement that drives the whole book, Jungwon's introduction, and the Lee and Yang families.

3. **Where you go, I go** · Ch 2 "funerals and weddings"
   *Her father named Haechan her protector until the wedding. Then Chairman Yang called him "the boy who burned my son," and she slapped him in the alley.*
   Why: Haechan as her guard, and the fire that supposedly killed Jungwon's brother Seojun.

4. **"Stay here, Solar."** · Ch 3 "the room across the hall"
   *Seven years under the same roof, and it was the first time he'd ever said her name.*
   Why: the slow-burn turn, Haechan's POV, and the wall between them cracking.

5. **The first kiss** · Ch 4 "the rules"
   *Angry, clumsy, furious. She bit his lip hard enough to bleed. Neither of them called it a mistake out loud.*
   Why: starts the secret romance under her father's roof.

6. ★ **Dal** · Ch 5 "near the water"
   *Dal, who did the crossword on the back step and first brought Haechan to her, was found on the mudflats with a cream card on his chest: "Yang. Lee."*
   Why: the first death and the start of the war beneath the truce. Dal's crossword clue ("a debt that cannot be repaid") keeps coming back.

7. ★ **The red dress** · Ch 19 "first dance"
   *Three hundred guests saw a lovely engagement party. Downstairs in the car park, she sewed Haechan shut while Jungwon held the lamp and stepped in front of his own father's gun.*
   Why: the end of Act I. Jungwon becomes an ally and a real third point of the triangle, and the Yangs learn about her stitching.

8. **The true thing** · Ch 20 "the true thing"
   *He never killed Seojun. Seven years ago he put him on a boat, burned and screaming, and never knew if he lived.*
   Why: the central twist, which reframes Haechan's guilt. In the same chapter Solar spends her first night with Jungwon. (Cut this if you'd rather players not get the twist.)

9. **Ten meters** · Ch 41 "ten meters"
   *"Ten meters, young master." / "Ten meters." The boy everyone said was the killer and the boy who lost a brother, carrying the dead together.*
   Why: Haechan and Jungwon as uneasy brothers-in-arms. Optional page for the trio dynamic.

10. ★ **The kimbap woman** · Ch 64 "before light" and Ch 65 "the witness"
    *Haechan finally saw the face behind the glove. When customs took the old kimbap woman, he and Sunghoon brought her home, and Solar, steady-handed, dressed her wrists by the stove.*
    Why: where the book stops (Feb 6, 66 days before the wedding). Seojun is alive and in play, customs is closing in, and tonight is the run.

## 3. HOAX (Hanlim High, through Ch 99)

1. ★ **Park Jihoon hate club** · Ch 1–2
   *The group chat was mostly roasting Jihoon, and he showed up anyway with ice cream. Her best friend since preschool, practically her parents' second kid.*
   Why: introduces Solar Kim, her friend group (Cherry, Asahi, Jaehyuk, Hyunsuk, Junkyu), and Jihoon. Her parents push her to run.

2. ★ **Nana vs. Maeumi** · Ch 3
   *A 6:30 AM dog walk, a ball over a fence, and a boy in a Hanlim uniform yelling at her to control her dog. "What a jerk," she thought.*
   Why: the meet-ugly with Jungwon, and the dogs (Nana and Maeumi) as a running bit.

3. ★ **The handshake** · Ch 16 "the handshake reveal" (with Ch 18)
   *First election anyone's ever contested. She stepped up to shake the student council president's hand and said "What?!" into the mic. It was the dog boy.*
   Why: sets up the election plot, Solar against Jungwon. His friends (Sunoo, Jake, Niki, Heeseung, Sunghoon, Jay) had known all along.

4. **Kidnapped by Jihoon** · Ch 25
   *A surprise hike and a skinny bridge over the river. He held her hand "so she wouldn't fall." She called him a weirdo and posted proof.*
   Why: Jihoon's hidden feelings and the start of the second side of the triangle.

5. **Doctor Jungwon** · Ch 42–43
   *She fell off a ladder decorating for homecoming, and the boy she was running against carried her home and nursed her all weekend. "I solemnly swear to possibly nurse you back to health."*
   Why: the enemies-to-something turn, and his "I'm sorry I ever treated you like that."

6. **The recording and the curb** · Ch 61–62
   *Homecoming, 1920s theme. A recording, a fight in the hallway, tears on the curb, and the person who sat down beside her was Jihoon. She kissed him.*
   Why: the homecoming fallout and the Jihoon kiss. It sets up the reveal that Jihoon and Cherry were behind the recording and notes (Ch 72).

7. ★ **President and Vice President** · Ch 71 "results day"
   *Jungwon won, and with the whole auditorium watching he picked her as his vice president. Backstage she told him she couldn't do this. Then she kissed him.*
   Why: the election result and the current titles. Their first kiss.

8. **Flour fight** · Ch 91
   *Heart cookies cut with a butter knife, a flour fight around the kitchen island, and this time he kissed her.*
   Why: their first real kiss initiated by Jungwon. It follows Jihoon's overdose (Ch 89) and the night Solar almost relapsed (Ch 90), which is the shadow over all of this.

9. ★ **i <3 u** · Ch 95 (bake sale)
   *Equations hidden in cupcakes, a chalk trail to a bench, and "i <3 u" at the end. Then flowers: "will you be my girlfriend?" (Also: a mystery cookie.)*
   Why: makes them official ("jolar"). The edibles thread is also here.

10. **Can't hide in the closet forever** · Ch 99
    *Exam week, lunch for two in the council room, and Danielle waiting with a video on her phone. Solar deleted it. Danielle didn't seem worried.*
    Why: where the book stops. Sophomore year, Danielle's video, Jihoon's addiction, and the conversation Jungwon is still waiting for are all unresolved.

*Note:* Cherry is her best friend ("my wife"), so give her at least a cameo in pages 1, 6 and 7.

## 4. Stay (Persona 3 itself: the game's main story)

Described generally, from the player's own run. Nothing from the epilogue. Source sections in `FEMC-REVAMP-LORE.md` are given in place of chapters.

1. ★ **Waking up in Tartarus** · Lore: "The premise", "Story shape: Prologue"
   *No name, no memories, already in a Gekkoukan uniform, and somehow she knew how to fight. Then Akihiko found her.*
   Why: where her life in this world starts, and Akihiko from the first moment.

2. ★ **Haenim** · Lore: "Her Persona: Haenim"
   *The first time she called her Persona, the name glitched. Lady Sun, veiled, carrying charms from lives Solar couldn't remember.*
   Why: her Persona and the mystery of the three charms (bell, jade ring, star pin).

3. ★ **Joining S.E.E.S.** · Lore: "The premise"
   *They took in a girl with no past, gave her an armband and a room at the dorm, and enrolled her at Gekkoukan.*
   Why: her found family and her place in Iwatodai.

4. **The Dark Hour** · Lore: "Who arrives, and why", "P3 in the background"
   *Every midnight the city turned green and silent and people became coffins. Then they climbed Tartarus.*
   Why: the core P3 setting for players who skipped ahead or need a refresher.

5. **The blank page** · Lore: "The blank page"
   *Elizabeth has a page for everyone who enters this world. Solar's was blank, and even Elizabeth didn't know why.*
   Why: the central mystery of who she is.

6. ★ **Full moons** · Lore: "P3 in the background"
   *One giant Shadow every full moon, and they faced every one of them together.*
   Why: the main-story backbone and the S.E.E.S. battles.

7. ★ **The bonds** · Lore: "Who arrives, and why", "Romances"
   *First the visitors from other games, then the searchers, people from her own lives who followed her missing phone number through the Dark Hour.*
   Why: the social links and the people she now stands to lose or keep.

8. **October 4th** (optional) · Lore: "P3 in the background" (Shinjiro on 10/4 stays canon)
   *Some full moons cost more than others.*
   Why: P3's canon loss and the weight S.E.E.S. carries. Keep it vague, or cut it if it feels like a spoiler for new players.
