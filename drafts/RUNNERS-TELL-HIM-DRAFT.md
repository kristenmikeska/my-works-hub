## 12/26 late · the terms (new event)

Place: Her Room, late night. After the terrace and the tour; the family is in Greenwich; the apartment has two people in it.

She couldn't sleep, which was nothing new, and he could, which was. He was on his back on the side of the bed nearest the door with one arm thrown over his eyes and his breathing gone long and even, and she lay on her side and watched the four hundred windows across the street go out one at a time, the way they did after one, like somebody walking down a hallway turning off lamps.

At 11:58 she reached for her phone. She did it without deciding to. She'd done it every night since the car, the way you check a stove you know you turned off.

11:59.

She watched it. She held her breath without meaning to and watched the number sit there, and nothing in the room changed, and the radiator ticked, and the minute turned over to 12:00 the way minutes do, and she let the breath out through her nose very slowly so it wouldn't make a sound.

> **haechan:** "What happens at 11:59?"

She nearly dropped the phone. He hadn't moved his arm. His voice came out from under it flat and clear and not at all asleep.

> **solar:** "...Nothing."

> **haechan:** "You've done it every night I've been here. Twice in Syracuse. You hold your breath."

> **solar:** "You're supposed to be asleep."

> **haechan:** "I'm supposed to be a lot of things."

He took his arm off his eyes. He didn't sit up. He turned his head on the pillow and looked at her in the light from the four hundred windows, the way he looked at a waveform he hadn't figured out yet, patient, not pushing.

> **haechan:** "We made rules. On the stairs at Alpha. You made me promise I'd tell you before I disappeared."

> **solar:** "That was about you."

> **haechan:** "Yeah. I noticed it was only about me."

**(choose)**

**A. (Tell him. All of it.)** *flag: epi-r-hc-told*

So she told him.

Not well. She'd told Jaehyun on Tuesday morning in the study, in order, like a deposition, and she'd thought that would make it easier the second time, and it didn't, because Jaehyun had listened like a man taking notes and Haechan listened like a man being told where the floor was going to give.

She told him the roof hadn't been the end of it. That it still came, smaller, quieter, in the shape of things forgetting her: the frame in the hall with the snow smooth where she should have been, the girls' group chat telling her she'd left at 3:12 in the morning, her own reflection in the lift half a second behind her. She told him about 11:59. That she checked because one night it was going to stay 11:59, and she'd know. That she thought she knew which night.

> **haechan:** "Which night."

> **solar:** "Midnight's the door. It's always been midnight. And the biggest midnight there is is in five days."

He didn't say anything for a long time. Long enough that she thought she'd broken something. Then he reached over in the dark, found her hand on the duvet without looking, and held it, not hard, the way you'd hold something you'd just been told was fragile and didn't want to prove it by squeezing.

> **haechan:** "Okay."

> **solar:** "Okay?"

> **haechan:** "Okay. Now I know. That's the only thing I wanted. I'd rather know where the floor's going to give than find out on my way down."

> **solar:** "You're not going to tell me it's going to be fine."

> **haechan:** "You'd know I was lying. You always know."

He pulled her in by the hand, and she let him. She put her face against his shoulder, where his T-shirt smelled like her mother's detergent now, which was a strange thing to have done to a person in two days, and felt him breathe in and hold it, and let it out slowly, matching hers on purpose until they were the same.

> **haechan:** "Tomorrow night you check it, I'm checking it with you. Every night. Out loud."

> **solar:** "That's insane."

> **haechan:** "It's a duet. You're good at those."

**B. "Go back to sleep, Haechan."** *flag: epi-r-hc-kept*

> **solar:** "Go back to sleep, Haechan."

He looked at her for a second longer. He didn't argue. He never argued when she shut a door; he just noted where it was.

> **haechan:** "Okay, princess."

He put his arm back over his eyes. She lay there and listened to him not fall asleep, and pretended not to know, and he pretended not to know she knew, and outside the four hundred windows went out one by one until there was only one left on, high on the left, someone else who couldn't sleep either.

She thought: *I'll tell him tomorrow.* She'd thought it in the car, and in the lift, and on the terrace with his coat around her. It was the easiest sentence in the world to think. It was the only one she never got to the end of.

## 12/27–12/28 early · the second pillow (new hangout, optional)

Hint: *It's snowing, nobody's home, and he hasn't moved in an hour.* Place: Her Room, morning. A plain, happy scene: no glitch, no dread on purpose.

It snowed all morning and neither of them got up for it.

She woke up at nine-forty, which was the latest she'd slept in that bed since she was eleven, with the room gone the soft gray-white that snow made of everything, and the radiator knocking, and the heaviest arm in New York across her waist. He was awake. He was reading. He had one of her old books held up over both of them in his other hand, the copy of *Anne of Green Gables* with her name in it in a nine-year-old's round handwriting, and he was halfway through.

> **solar:** "Where did you get that."

> **haechan:** "Your shelf. You underlined things."

> **solar:** "I was nine."

> **haechan:** "You underlined *I'm so glad I live in a world where there are Octobers.* Twice. Different pens."

> **solar:** "Give it back."

> **haechan:** "No. I'm at the part with the hair dye."

She tried to take it. He held it higher, out of reach, without even looking away from the page, and she ended up half on top of him with her hand around his wrist, laughing into his neck, and he turned the page with his thumb, one-handed, very calmly, like a man who had all the time in the world and intended to spend it here.

**(choose)**

**A. (Let him read. Fall back asleep on him.)**
She gave up. She put her head down on his chest instead and listened to him turn pages, slow and even, a sound she'd never once thought of as a sound until right now, and somewhere around the hair dye she fell asleep again, the second time in one morning, which she hadn't done since she was a child. When she woke up the book was face-down on his stomach and he was asleep too, his mouth a little open, snow still coming down past the window, the room the warmest she had ever known it.

**B. "Read it to me."**
He looked at her over the top of the book like she'd asked him to sing. Then he cleared his throat and read it to her, flat, in the exact voice he used to tell Nadia she was rushing the *and*, and it was so funny she had to put her face in the pillow, and he didn't stop, he read the whole chapter in that voice with total commitment, and somewhere in the middle she stopped laughing and just listened, because under the flat there was something careful, the way he slowed down for the lines she'd underlined, every one of them, without being asked.

*(both)* They didn't get up until eleven. It was, she'd decide later, the morning she'd keep if she could only keep one.

## 12/30 late · the hang-up (new event, if told)

Place: Her Room, late night. He's in Bushwick on Theo's couch. She calls him, or he calls her.

He called at 11:51, which meant he'd done the math.

> **haechan:** "You in bed?"

> **solar:** "Yes."

> **haechan:** "Phone on the pillow. Not in your hand. On the pillow, by your ear."

She did it. It was ridiculous. She lay on her side with the phone on the pillow next to her face like a person, and his voice came out of it small and close and a little tinny, and behind it she could hear Bushwick: a radiator worse than hers, a bus downshifting, somebody's television through a wall, and once, very far off, a dog with an opinion.

They talked about nothing. The session, which had gone long. Nadia's bridge, which was fixed. Theo's couch, which had a spring in it in exactly the place a person's spine went. Hyein's *forty-one* outfit options for tomorrow, which Hanni had narrowed to three, which Hyein had then expanded to nine.

At 11:58 he stopped talking.

> **haechan:** "Okay. Count with me."

> **solar:** "Haechan—"

> **haechan:** "Out loud. I'm right here."

They counted the last minute down together, out loud, on a phone, him on a broken couch in Brooklyn and her on the eleventh floor of a building on Fifth, his voice a quarter-second behind hers down the line, so that she heard every number twice. At *one* she shut her eyes.

The phone said 12:00.

> **haechan:** "There it goes."

> **solar:** "There it goes."

Neither of them hung up. She meant to. She meant to say goodnight and press the red button like a normal person, and instead she lay there listening to him breathe, and somewhere in the next twenty minutes his breathing went long and even and slow, and she realised he'd fallen asleep with the phone on his own pillow on the other side of the river.

She didn't hang up. She lay in the dark and listened to Haechan sleep in Brooklyn, and the dog with the opinion, and the bus, and she fell asleep like that, the call still connected, two hours and fourteen minutes on the screen in the morning, which she screenshotted, and never told him she had.

## 12/30 late · eleven fifty-nine (new event, if kept)

Place: Her Room, late night. He's come back from Bushwick for the night, uninvited, because he's been watching her for four days. This is the fight.

He was sitting on the window seat when she came out of the bathroom, in his coat still, with his elbows on his knees, and he didn't say hi.

> **haechan:** "You looked at your phone at 11:59 on Friday. Saturday. Sunday. Last night in the lift, on the stairs at the reception, in the dark kitchen with your brother. Every night. You hold your breath. When it turns over you let it out like you've been underwater."

> **solar:** "Haechan."

> **haechan:** "Marco didn't know you this morning. I watched it from the lobby. I was coming in with coffee and I watched the doorman who's known you since you were seven ask you who you were visiting, and I watched your face, and you came out and you stood under the awning for nine minutes and you didn't call me."

> **solar:** "It was nothing. He was tired, he said, the dog—"

> **haechan:** "Don't."

He'd never once said it like that. Not loud. He didn't get loud. It was worse than loud: it was the voice he used in the studio when a take was ruined and everybody knew it and nobody wanted to say.

> **haechan:** "On the stairs at Alpha you made me promise I'd tell you before I disappeared. Before I used. You made me say it out loud. And I meant it, I've kept it, I told you about Thursdays, I told you about my mother, I've told you every single bad thing I've got."

> **haechan:** "And the whole time you were the one disappearing."

The room went very quiet. The four hundred windows, the radiator, the snow.

> **solar:** "That's not fair."

> **haechan:** "No. It isn't."

**(choose)**

**A. "Because if I said it out loud it would be true."**
It came out before she could stop it, small and cracked, the truest thing she'd said all week. He looked at her. His jaw did the thing. And then all the hard went out of his shoulders at once, like a cable cut.

> **haechan:** "It's true anyway, Solar. It's just true by yourself."

**B. "You'd have tried to fix it. You can't fix this."**

> **haechan:** "I know I can't fix it."

> **haechan:** "I wasn't going to fix it. I was going to be there for it. That's different. You of all people know that's different."

She knew. That was the worst part. She'd said almost exactly that to him in October about something else, and he'd remembered, and he'd waited two months to hand it back to her at the exact moment it would land.

*(both)* So she told him. Sitting on the floor of her childhood bedroom with her back against the bed and him on the window seat above her, not touching, the whole thing: the frame in the hall, the lift, the group chat, Marco, 11:59, midnight being the door, New Year's being the biggest midnight there was. *flag: epi-r-hc-told-late*

He listened to all of it without interrupting. When she got to the end, he got down off the window seat, slowly, and sat on the floor next to her, shoulder to shoulder, and didn't say anything for a long time.

> **haechan:** "I'm still mad."

> **solar:** "I know."

> **haechan:** "I'm going to be mad for like a day. Then I'm going to be here. Both. At the same time. You're going to have to deal with it."

> **solar:** "Okay."

> **haechan:** "Tomorrow night you check it, I'm checking it with you. Out loud. That's the deal now. New deal."

She leaned her head on his shoulder. He let her. After a minute his hand came up and found the back of her neck, under her hair, and stayed there, still a little angry, holding on anyway.
