# Persona 3: Equinox · the plan

The sequel to *Persona 3: Solstice*. This is the shape of the game only: the calendar, the cast, the systems, the save, the build order and the art. No story. Everything here can change once you answer the questions at the bottom.

---

## What it is

- Its own game, with its own title screen, logo and save.
- Solar is the main character again.
- A full year, the same size as Solstice: a day-by-day life sim plus a big dungeon.
- It opens the way you already know: everyone wakes up in the Personaverse and remembers each other, and Solar has been taken.
- It opens the same way whichever ending or epilogue you finished.

## When and where

- **The year:** April 2010 to the first week of March 2011 in game time, the year after Solstice. It ends with graduation and the finale, like Solstice.
- **The town:** mostly Iwatodai and Port Island again, so most of Solstice's backgrounds come back. There are a few new places in town, and a new dungeon.
- **School:** Solar is back at Gekkoukan for the school year. Exams, festivals, holidays and a graduation at the end.
- **The rhythm:** the same as Solstice. School days and days off, After School, Evening, Late Night, and the Dark Hour. A full moon every month with a big fight. Weather and seasons as before.
- **Arrivals:** not everyone is there on day one. People keep arriving through the year, the way the searchers did in Solstice.

## Starting a game

- **New Game:** start fresh.
- **Import from Solstice:** pick a finished Solstice save. It brings over your looks only:
  - every Wardrobe outfit you own, for every fighter
  - weapon looks, Evoker looks, battle themes and menu colours
  - titles
  - wallpapers
  - the album (CGs, polaroids, photo booth strips)
  - leftover Velvet Plumes
- It also reads which epilogue you finished, and which route, so a few lines in the opening match it. A fresh start gets neutral lines. The game is the same either way.
- **What doesn't come over:** levels, links, skills, Personas and Echoes, money, items.
- The Solstice save is only read, never changed.

## Systems coming back from Solstice

- **Calendar and daily life:** slots, weather, the five-day forecast, holidays, commute choices.
- **Social links:** ranks to 10, and to 15 for the main ones, perks, texts, holidays, hangouts, late nights, romance and friendship routes.
- **Social stats:** Knowledge, Courage, Proficiency, Charm, Kindness.
- **The phone:** Messages, Who's free, Jobs, Contacts, calls and messages, group chats, stickers.
- **Jobs:** with raises and rare rewards.
- **Go Out map** and places.
- **Battle:** weaknesses, One More, All-Out Attacks, Theurgy, Shuffle Time, cards, Rush.
- **Haenim's sun** and the party skill trees.
- **The Velvet Room:** Elizabeth, Echoes, fusion, the Compendium, respec, requests.
- **Wardrobe, fitting room and Boutique:** outfits, banners, pulls.
- **Photo booth** and the album.
- **Minigames:** the ones already built, used in the story where they fit.
- **Velvet Trials** (the boss rush).
- **The Index, Theater, Tips, settings, save slots.**
- **New Game+,** with a deeper climb past the bottom of the new dungeon.

## New systems

- **The Well:** a new dungeon. It goes down instead of up. Blocks, floors, guardians, teleport gates, events and the Reaper, like Tartarus, with new looks and new Shadows.
- **Reflections:** some rooms in the Well have reflections in them instead of normal Shadows.
- **Home floors:** some floors open onto places from home (Syracuse, Incheon, Hanlim), using the epilogue backgrounds.
- **Asks:** people ask Solar for things all year. How she answers matters, for them and for the ending.
- **The Thread:** all of Solar's bonds added together, shown as one meter.
- **Parents as links:** six new links, with scenes where the families meet.
- **The stage:** LE SSERAFIM performances, using the booth rhythm game. You chart the songs.
- **A second navigator** in the Well.
- **A duo attack** for one pair of fighters, late in the year.
- **New memory cards:** a new set for this year.
- **A new Echo set.**
