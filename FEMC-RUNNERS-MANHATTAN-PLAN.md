**Why "held" works:** up to the rooftop, the glitches are Iwatodai leaking into Syracuse. After the rooftop they turn around: she's still here, but the world is erasing her a little at a time. That gives the horror somewhere new to go. It also gives Jaehyun's porch line its weight: *"Then I'll come and get you. Wherever it is. I did it once."*

### The Lee home (taken from Better Photography, parts 24–28)
- **The building:** Upper East Side, on Fifth in the Seventies. A marble lobby with a white flower arrangement "the size of a car". **Marco** at the front desk calls her "Miss Lee". The elevator takes 11 seconds and has a bench and a mirror. There's a service lift, and a wreath on the door at Christmas.
- **The apartment:** eleven rooms on the **eleventh floor**, redecorated in October.
  - A 40 ft hall; her room is the fourth door, after Jaehyun's.
  - **Her room:** a window seat, curtains left open onto a building with about 400 windows.
  - The study (her mother's, 4,000 books, the awards).
  - The dining room, which seats 12–14 and sounds terrible.
  - Two front rooms with the "good floor".
  - The kitchen, with its wonky left ring and her mother's terrible, expensive coffee machine.
  - **The black grand piano** under the front-room windows. She had lessons from 6 to 11 and hasn't played it since about 2018. "Nothing on the piano."
- **The roof terrace:** through a cupboard-like door past the kitchen, then a stair with a timer light, then a barred door you shoulder open. Heaters, evergreen planters, a pergola, the park and the skyline. **It's Solar's secret place since she was 12, and she has never brought anyone.**
- **People:**
  - **Mrs. Oh,** the housekeeper of 11 years, there until 8. She has lied to Solji's face for Solar before.
  - **Ms. Han,** her father's assistant of eleven years (Kat, Nov 5). She sends the PDF schedules.
  - Mother **Solji**, father **Jaesang** (Lee Industries), and Jaehyun.
  - Family in Greenwich: Halmeoni, Aunt Christine, Uncle Sangchul, cousin Seojin.
  - **Aunt Mira,** Jaesang's cut-off sister ("She looks like Mira").
  - The **Kaplans** host the parents' New Year's Eve.

**What stays the same:** Dec 1–20 keeps everything already built: the home pass, the time pass, Regionals, the studio, the Dec 19 footage and the fights. Only the rooftop ending changes, along with the longer parties in section 2.
