# Runners epilogue: making it longer, with more context

An audit of the finished Runners epilogue (Dec 1 to Dec 20, Syracuse) and a menu of additions. Nothing here has been
written into the game yet. Tick the boxes you want and the next pass will write them.

Word counts are rough estimates for new writing. For scale, the epilogue is about 80,000 words now, a typical hangout runs
400 to 1,000 words (about 600 on average), and a story scene runs 500 to 4,000.

---

## The short version

- **There isn't enough time to see the hangouts.** A run has **17 free After Class / Evening times where someone is
  actually around**, and there are **32 hangouts on offer in any one run** (35 written; Haechan has 7, but a run only
  gets 4 of them). So even a perfect run sees **about half (17 of 32)**. A normal run, where you also go to the café,
  study or stay in now and then, sees **about 12 to 15 (40 to 45%)**.
- **Week 1 is the worst.** There are 3 free times for 9 hangouts. Mark's "no notes", Jaehyun's "treatment" and Chenle's
  "forty-one for forty-one" all only work on Friday Dec 5 After Class, so **you can only ever see one of the three.**
- **The main problem is time, not scenes.** Most people have 3 or 4 hangouts, which is a fine number. But there's room for
  only about half of what's already written. Adding scenes without adding time would make it worse. The fix is both:
  first more time (Section C), then new hangouts to fill it (Section B).
- **The thinnest stretch is Dec 18 to 19, before the holiday party.** The girls work out "it's yours" that morning, the
  bell tower happens at midnight, and then the next thing you see is 8:51 p.m. at Sigma. Everything in between (the
  morning after, her last final, getting ready, the plan for the night) is only mentioned later as something that already
  happened.

## C. More time

Right now a run has 40 After Class and Evening times across the 20 days. Story scenes take 15 and practice takes 5
(Dec 2, 4, 9, 13 and 20), which leaves 20. Three of those (Dec 1, Dec 7 and Dec 20 Evenings) have nobody to see, so
17 are real hangout times. Morning and Late Night are never free.

Ways to make room, from most to least useful:

- [ ] **C1. Late Night hangouts on quiet nights** (about +8 to +10 times). On nights with no story scene (Dec 3, 8, 9, 10,
  11, 12, 13, 15 and 17), Late Night offers people before Sleep. Runners is a night story, so these are where the
  roofs, porches, 1 a.m. studio sessions and sleepovers would go. Fight nights and story nights stay as they are.
  *Trade-off:* needs a small game change, since Late Night is only Sleep now. The night before Regionals (Dec 5) should
  stay closed.
- [ ] **C2. Give the empty times people** (+3 real times, no game change). Dec 1 Evening (welcome back), Dec 20 Evening
  (goodbyes) and Dec 20 After Class (the last practice as a real scene). These times already exist; they just have
  nobody in them.
- [ ] **C3. Make the two practices after Regionals optional** (+2 times on Dec 9 and 13). Doyoung said no practice the day
  after, the whiteboard is wiped and the season's next stage is months away. Practice becomes a choice in Go Out: practice
  (the stat bonus) or see someone. *Trade-off:* small game change; a bit less of that stat. Regionals week and the Dec 20
  practice stay required.
- [ ] **C4. Free weekend daytime** (+2 times: Saturday Dec 13 and Sunday Dec 14 mornings). Brunch-type hangouts.
  *Trade-off:* small game change; only two days benefit.
- [ ] **C5. Longer windows for week 1's hangouts** (no game change). Week 1's scenes now end on Dec 5. Letting the quiet
  ones (Mark's, Jaehyun's, Chenle's, Jaemin's) run until Dec 10 means you don't lose them to the Friday crunch. *Trade-off:*
  only helps once there's more time in week 2; a couple of lines would need a "we never got to" touch.
- [ ] **C6. A few hangouts for two** (no game change). Hanni and Hyein together, or Chenle and Minji in the lab, so one time
  shows two people. *Trade-off:* you'd get closer to both at once, but it's less one-on-one.
- [ ] **C7. Fix Dec 7 Evening** (gap 14). Removes a time that was never real. Listed so the numbers are honest.
- [ ] **C8. An extra week.** *Not recommended.* The act is pinned between her coming home on Dec 1 and the solstice roof
  on Dec 20, with the car on the 21st. An extra week would mean moving Regionals or finals, or putting something after
  the roof, and the act's shape depends on those dates.

### Recommendation

Do **C1 + C2 + C3 + C5 + C7**. That takes a run from **17 real hangout times to about 30**. Then write **12 to 16 new
hangouts**, putting the goodbyes, the Late Night ones and the gap-fillers first. With about 45 to 48 hangouts on offer
and about 30 times to play them, a run sees **roughly 60 to 65%** of them. That's enough that it no longer feels like there
wasn't enough time, while a second run still has things you didn't see. C4 and C6 are nice extras if you want even more.

Do Section A's gaps 1 to 5 (the run-up to the party) no matter what, since they don't depend on any of this.
