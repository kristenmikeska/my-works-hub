**How to use this list**
- Attach the files on each item's **Attach** line, in that order, then paste the prompt. **Every attachment is in `Femc Mode Images/Place Prompt Attachments/`**, named after its item.
- Save finished art in **New Adds** under its **Save as** name and tell Claude.
- **ChatGPT** for anything with a face or a logo, **Gemini** for places with no people.
- **Equinox's palette for every prompt:** blood red, dark oxblood and near-black, with bone-white light. No pink-heavy colours (that's Solstice), no blue, navy, indigo, purple, lilac or green; gold only as a tiny accent. It's already written into each prompt. Characters' own clothes can still have their own colours.
- **The mood (Kat, Nov 15):** Equinox is gloomier and darker than Solstice, with horror and creepiness. The Dark Hour is blood red, not green. If a picture comes back too bright or friendly, ask for "darker, gloomier, more horror: deeper shadows, more red-black, something unsettling".
- **No background = transparent (Kat, Nov 15):** pose sheets, poses, portraits, skill effects, stickers, logos, emblems and anything else without a scene behind it are always made on a **transparent** background, never white or black. If one comes back with a white or black fill, ask for "the same picture on a fully transparent background".
- Solar's clothes in a prompt are only a suggestion: change her outfit or hair whenever you like.
