# Stay epilogue: questions for Kat

Spoiler-free: these ask about your preferences and canon in the abstract, never about what happens. Answer any you like;
anything unanswered stays as it is now.

## Your preferences

1. **Friends outside the dorm.** Right now everyone in the Stay epilogue lives in the dorm. Would you like the year's other
   friends to turn up over spring break too (for example Rio, Saori, Kenta, Ann, Kasumi, Haru, Kuromi, or Kat and Ethan),
   each only if you met them that year? If yes, which ones matter most to you?
2. **A rainy day.** The fortnight is now fair and dry, because most of the outdoor hangouts can land on any day of their
   window and would read strangely in rain. Would you like one rainy day anyway (it would mean a few rain versions of lines,
   like Runners got for snow)? Any day you'd want it on?
3. **Koromaru.** He shows up more now, as the small recurring touch (like Nana in HOAX). Would you like him to have his own
   bond (his own name on the Go Out list), or keep him sharing Ken's?
4. **Pictures.** Three optional CGs are in `ART-ADDS-stay.md` (S1-S3). Want any of them, or a different moment?
5. **Romance heat.** How warm should Stay's romance scenes be: sweet and cutting away early, or warmer, like Runners?
